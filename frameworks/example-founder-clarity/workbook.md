# Founder Clarity Workbook

Progress tracker for the Founder Clarity Framework. Questions are worked through naturally in sessions — 1 to 3 per session. Claude captures your answers and marks progress as you go.

**Status key:** `[ ]` not started · `[~]` discussed, answer in progress · `[x]` answered

---

## Module 1: Vision and Direction

- [ ] **Q1.** What are you building — and who is it for? Describe the world that exists when this works.
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q2.** What does winning look like in five years — specifically? (Revenue, team, market position, lifestyle, impact.)
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q3.** Why does this have to be you? What makes you the right person to build this?
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q4.** Is the vision you started with still the vision you want to build toward — or has it shifted?
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q5.** What would you be building if you knew you couldn't fail?
  > *Answer captured during session*

## Module 2: Decisions and Clarity

- [ ] **Q1.** What decision have you been avoiding — and what's it actually costing you to not make it?
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q2.** Walk me through the last big decision you made well. What made it work?
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q3.** Where in your business are you holding decisions that should be delegated?
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q4.** What would you decide, right now, if you knew you could course-correct later?
  > *Answer captured during session*

- [ ] **Q5.** What's one thing you already know the answer to — but haven't committed to yet?
  > *Answer captured during session*
