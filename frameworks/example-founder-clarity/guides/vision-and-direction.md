# Vision and Direction

## What This Guide Is For

When a founder loses clarity on direction, output doesn't stall — it fragments. They stay busy doing things that don't compound. This guide helps surface and sharpen what they're actually building toward, and why.

---

## The Three Clarity Questions

These are not goal-setting questions. They're orientation questions. Ask them slowly, one at a time.

**1. What are you building — and for whom?**
Not the elevator pitch. The real answer. Who is the world that exists when this works? What is materially different for the people you serve?

**2. What does winning look like in five years — specifically?**
Push past vague ("we're successful") to concrete. Revenue, team size, market position, lifestyle, impact. Specificity reveals whether the vision is real or aspirational.

**3. Why does this have to be you?**
This is the hardest question. Not "why are you qualified" — why does the world need *you* to do this? The answer to this question is often where purpose lives.

---

## The Clarity Check

When direction feels foggy, run this check:

- **Are you optimizing for the right thing?** (Revenue, growth, impact, learning — which one is actually driving decisions right now? Is that the right one?)
- **Is the vision still true?** (Founders' visions evolve. An outdated vision is a navigation system pointing the wrong way.)
- **What would you do if you knew you couldn't fail?** (Strips away fear-based constraints that masquerade as strategic ones.)

---

## Coaching Notes

- Vagueness in vision is almost always a sign of avoidance, not uncertainty. Push gently.
- "I don't know" often means "I'm afraid to say." Wait it out.
- If their current goals don't connect to their stated vision, name it.
- Vision drift is real — founders often don't notice when they've stopped building what they originally intended.

---

## Questions to Use in Session

- What are you building toward, beyond the next milestone?
- If this company became exactly what you imagined — what would that look like?
- What would you regret not building?
- Is the direction you're heading still the direction you actually want to go?
- When was the last time your vision felt fully alive? What was happening then?
