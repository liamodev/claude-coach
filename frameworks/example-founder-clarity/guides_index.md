# Founder Clarity Framework — Guides Index

Claude reads this index first, then reads the specific guide, then coaches from that material.

---

**File:** guides/vision-and-direction.md
**Summary:** Tools for getting clear on long-term direction, purpose, and what success actually means.
**When To Use:** When the client feels scattered, lacks clarity on where they're heading, is questioning whether they're building the right thing, or needs to reconnect with their "why."

---

**File:** guides/decision-filter.md
**Summary:** A five-question filter for making hard calls — especially when multiple options feel valid or fear is clouding judgment.
**When To Use:** When the client is stuck on a decision, going in circles, second-guessing a call they've already made, or avoiding a decision they know they need to make.

---

**File:** guides/energy-and-rhythm.md
**Summary:** A framework for protecting founder energy — identifying drains, designing better rhythms, and recovering from overextension.
**When To Use:** When the client is burned out, overwhelmed, running on empty, or when their output quality is declining. Also when they're about to enter a high-demand period.

---

**File:** guides/beliefs-and-identity.md
**Summary:** A structured approach to surfacing limiting beliefs, testing them against reality, and building the identity required for the next level.
**When To Use:** When the client is self-sabotaging, avoiding action they claim to want, shrinking from visibility or opportunity, or when the same pattern keeps repeating despite tactical changes.
