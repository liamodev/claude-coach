# The Essentials of Leadership

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Twenty-five essentials of leadership as you grow and strive.

1. **Priority Decision Making** — Decide on the important, not the convenient. Prioritize decisions require deep thought. Successful people make decisions quickly and change them rarely; unsuccessful people make decisions slowly and change them often.

2. **Develop an Iron Will** — Never surrender your leadership. Leadership is lifelong. Success is usually past the point of giving up. A sense of destiny is an imperative quality.

3. **Choose a Cause** — Look for growth. Examine for permanence. Make it simple. Be sure it's big enough to exchange your life for.

4. **A Plan to Obtain** — Very clearly defined; continuous checkpoints for assessment; expandable; your own plan.

5. **Elements of Power** — Money, knowledge, emotion, violence (understand and use rightly).

6. **The Ability** — Continuously develop capacity. Do not commit to what you do not understand. Know more than you need to. Gather people with talents you do not possess.

7. **Transaction Certainty** — You are trading your life, your reputation, the lives of others, and against other opportunities.

8. **Clarify** — Find out what has been going on and who is responsible. Establish yourself as the person in charge. Select two or three people you can depend on. Set ground rules for action.

9. **Belief in Direction** — Prepare a goals program with time frames. Get feedback. Refine for action. Create a working, sloganized mission statement.

10. **Perception** — Study every facet of real or perceived importance. List problems, opportunities, unknowns. Test assumptions against people and projects. Feed opportunities, solve problems, understand unknowns.

11. **Self Confidence** — Under commit and over perform. Speak, walk, act, perform without vacillating. Give your word carefully and keep it always. If you must discipline, do it quietly, firmly, quickly.

12. **Sense of Urgency** — Work to a deadline. Seek shortcuts without losing quality. Never lose ground. Always create team spirit.

13. **Energy** — Keep personally fit. Never let anyone see you tired, worried, or sick. Develop inspirational dissatisfaction. Remove superfluous baggage.

14. **Enthusiasm** — Reward performance (even your own). Develop an affirmation card. Always work towards and expect the best. Fan the flame of excitement.

15. **Understanding Human Nature** — Make others feel important. Give security. Make others part of the plan. Give direct credit for good performance.

16. **Power of Persuasion** — Never accept that you are in a non-negotiable situation. Display the big picture. Feel real or perceived need. Tone of voice fits the occasion.

17. **Timing** — Do not force a time frame that creates unbalanced penalties. Test time frames for reserves. Don't push after an inflexible time frame has expired. Condense time frames where possible.

18. **Courage** — Boldness, boldness, and boldness. Aim high and plan well. Use fear as a provocateur for courage. Create a fear list.

19. **Uncomplicated Principles** — Develop straight-line thinking. Be honest with others. Never lie or kid yourself. Develop a personal moral code.

20. **Stretch Others** — Do not expect unless you inspect. Encourage greatness. Keep encouragement and praise flowing. Get people out of their comfort zones.

21. **Handle a Crisis** — A crisis is an opportunity to gain ground. Do not repeat mistakes. Crisis (Asiatic) = danger + opportunity. Keep a positive mental attitude.

22. **Handle Pressure** — Accept pressure as a wise friend. Use pressure as adrenaline. Put yourself under pressure occasionally to solve difficult problems. Sometimes ignore pressure completely and it will disappear.

23. **Dislocate from Unpleasant Events or Failures** — Never keep a list of hurts. Read biographies of genuine achievers constantly. Change mental state with entertainment or solitude. Pray and thank God for blessings.

24. **Salesmanship** — The best way to sell is to ask questions. Tell the truth attractively. Deal only with those who can actually buy. Demonstrate by using your own products.

25. **Selection of People** — Choose those who already work hard. After major failure, some are in a better mental state to learn. Only employ positive mental attitude. Select those prepared to be rewarded on results, not effort.
