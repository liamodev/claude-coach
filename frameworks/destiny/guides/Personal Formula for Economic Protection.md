# Personal Formula for Economic Protection

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Ten simple principles that will protect you and look after your economics for your whole life.

1. Get out of debt as soon as practical and stay out.

2. Never put yourself in a situation where your financial future depends absolutely on borrowed money.

3. Never get involved in a loan, lease, or commitment where debt can expand.

4. Never enter into any agreement without personal control.

5. Build a reserve of cash and precious metals exclusive of other commitments.

6. Never allow reserves to become collateral.

7. Do not allow anyone to have control of your finances.

8. Keep a minimum of 20% of your net assets in cash and precious metals.

9. Never permit easy access to your assets.

10. Build a heritage for your family.
