# The Threshold of Performance

**Source:** Lesson 3 — The Commitment

The dream that you developed into a vision must now pass through the commitment threshold. Tenacity and perception in partnership with discipline and effort will be essential to produce the end results.

**Do not get into the theory trap** — creating great plans and developing good strategies in lieu of personal exposure to the pain of performance.

- **Pain is weakness escaping.** Strength and direction will replace your anxieties and reluctance with results.
- Choose several areas of actual direct personal involvement that would strengthen your abilities.
- Accept total responsibility for success or failure of the outcome.
- Expose yourself continually to scrutiny and feel the pain of growth.

The words "surrender," "giving up," and "procrastination" are no longer part of your vocabulary. Subterfuge and meaningless activities are no longer your calling.

It is difficult to cross this barrier of commitment for a lifetime, but once you are on the other side, committed to your mission, your life takes on a new meaning. The indecisions and retreating of the past are gone; the value of the life has equaled the value of the commitment.

**Practical tip:** Keep a simple card in your wallet with your written vision and the attributes of this program to keep you on course.
