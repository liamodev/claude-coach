# Eight Steps for Corporate Mission Statement

**Source:** Lesson 5 — The Mission

Order differs from personal mission statement; vehicle predates the goal. The corporate mission statement is shorter because it is supported by a major goals plan.

1. **The Corporate Vehicle** — The product/service as primary reason for being. Decide limits: e.g. kitchenware vs. home kitchen supply vs. engineering and design; fuel vs. energy business. *Suggested: "The corporation has been established and will continue for the production and the marketing of [e.g. energy]."*

2. **The Corporate Goal** — Time frame, size, variables. Set and agreed by leaders, confirmed by Board. Document in short-, medium-, and long-range planning. *Suggested: "Our goal is to lead in the production of quality and diversified [product] that will meet the market needs of a global economy."*

3. **The Corporate Commitment** — Discipline in equal measure; spelled out so all can grasp. Motivational tool throughout the corporation. *Suggested: "The commitment to satisfy our corporate goal will be reflected in our effort and purpose and sustained by internal and external examination."*

4. **The Corporate Qualification** — Proven capable people and attainments at every level. Quality at every level. Personnel upgraded for front line of new discoveries. *Suggested: "Our personnel, product and service will accept as evidence of qualification the results in the marketplace."*

5. **The Corporate Positioning** — Crucial point of timing and location of opportunities. Perception of advantage equal to fact. Industry, science, government discussions; in-depth investigations. *Suggested: "Our insightful projections and effective communication will provide many leadership advantages."*

6. **The Corporate Destination** — Clear goal as final destination. Practical purpose and growth; room for growth and variables for people, products, services. *Suggested: "Understanding that progress ultimately brings change, we will refocus our corporate goals, thereby stimulating all who participate towards that target."*

7. **Corporate Assessment** — Accomplishment and profit, in that order. Variable assessment criteria: market advantage, production, services, financial expenditure and return. *Suggested: "The performance of our product, personnel and investment will be regularly assessed to enable us to address inadequacies."*

8. **The Corporate Creed** — Substantial statement of integrity; morality that governs action. *Suggested: "All corporate activities will respect the ethics of confidence, clarity, and truth, and depend on all those involved to respond with loyalty to the corporate goals and mission."*
