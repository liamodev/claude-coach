# Formula to Measure Daily Performance

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

A mathematical formula to measure your daily performance (100 points per working day). We need to measure performance because often it's based on how we feel rather than what actually took place.

## Area 1 — Simplicity (25 Points)

- **Organization:** Were you organized for the day and throughout the day, or did you allow circumstances, events, or other people to reprogram your day?
- **Habits:** Were your habits controlled and in keeping with your goals, or did you break down in any area?
- **Clarity:** Did you have clarity in what you were about, or were you confused or frustrated?
- **Assessment:** Did you allow space for reconstruction and assessment and prevent everything landing on you at once?

Keep life simple. Do not get hung up with gadgets and superfluous material items. (Example: go through wardrobe every 12 weeks; give away unworn items; take very few extras when traveling.)

## Area 2 — Desperation Quotient (25 Points)

Am I a desperate person? Do I believe that if I were desperate enough I could achieve more? Sometimes means a move into the pain zone.

- **Intensity:** Pay attention to detail.
- **Urgency:** Everything you are involved in must contribute to the speed at which the goal is reached.
- **Commitment:** The effort required to get a particular commitment fulfilled.
- **Sacrifice:** Something for nothing does not exist. To do something in one direction you must let go of something else. Have courage to give up as well as to take on.

## Area 3 — Planning (25 Points)

- **Life:** Whole life plan (e.g. until 85th birthday); watch for milestones.
- **Structure:** Segmentation through the whole structure so you can handle it correctly.
- **Feedback:** Give feedback to ensure you are on course and that planning is accurate and true.
- **Daily Planning:** Use petitioning in all areas to evaluate what you are doing.

## Area 4 — Action (25 Points)

- **Routine:** Routine tasks every day; done diligently and well; measured.
- **Projects:** Projects that demand your attention; do not let go; monitor regularly.
- **People:** You cannot do anything without people. Relate to them; put a measurement on relationships; consider how to expand people power.
- **Profits:** Activity without objectivity is pointless. Object of business is service and profit. Monitor profits daily; give yourself a 1–25 score.
