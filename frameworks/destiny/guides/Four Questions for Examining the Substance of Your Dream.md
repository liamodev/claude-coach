# Four Questions for Examining the Substance of Your Dream

**Source:** Lesson 1 — The Dream

Use these four simple questions to examine the substance of your dream.

1. Do you want to impact your area, your state, your country, your region, or the whole world?

2. By what means will you express that impact?

3. How will you accurately measure its effect?

4. By what method can you guarantee the continuance or permanence of your life's work?

**The acid test of the dream:** Its overwhelming, all-encompassing, and demonstrable response from the possessor.

Serious dreams are the substance of greatness. Evaluate dreams in terms of **passion, perseverance, and permanence**. Their greatness can be measured by their impact.
