# Formula Describing the Nature of Entrepreneurs

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Use this as a backdrop against your own life. Entrepreneurs are very different people.

1. They have drive and will keep on going when everyone else has given up and gone home.

2. They are persuasive in getting others to see and follow through on their point of view.

3. They have perception and can see what is not distinguishable to others and believe what others find unbelievable.

4. They have strong ego needs that compel them to win, sometimes at any cost.

5. They can respond to failure and crisis with optimism.

6. They have confidence in their own ability to adjust and win in changing circumstances.

7. They know what their inner beliefs will permit them to do.

8. They plan strategies and measure their progress continually.

9. They have a short-term view when assessing performance and a long-term view when assessing success.

10. Their final destination has clear, measurable, and specific time, quality, and quantity values.
