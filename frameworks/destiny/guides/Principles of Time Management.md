# Principles of Time Management

**Source:** Lesson 2 — The Vision

Six principles of time management to establish a vision and get more done with time left over.

1. **A simple lifestyle.** Create a lifestyle and structure-free organization that is simple.
2. **Concentrate on essentials.** Get rid of excess baggage that limits effectiveness and burns time and energy.
3. **Control your time.** Your life is a time zone that is exclusively yours. Control it.
4. **Apply strong pressure to specific tasks.** Evaluate and concentrate on your final destination.
5. **Choose words carefully and accurately to save time.** Get into the habit of saying things clearly once.
6. **Time alone and time at work are of equal value.** Solitude for reflection, imagination, and planning is as important as visible work.

Taking charge of your time will create a measurement of life's value and reflect your assessment of the great gift called life.
