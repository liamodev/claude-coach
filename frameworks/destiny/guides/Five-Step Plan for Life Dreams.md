# Five-Step Plan for Life Dreams

**Source:** Lesson 1 — The Dream

If you have lost the capacity to dream about your future and the incredible possibilities you can achieve, use this five-step plan.

1. **Read Biographies:** Read biographies of great achievers. If you have difficulty reading, obtain audiobooks. Make notes from them towards your own destiny.

2. **Embrace Courage:** Embrace courage as your friend and put your life up for scrutiny by becoming involved in those things which you have thus far feared to do. Expose yourself to situations that will stretch your mental courage and capacity.

3. **Consider a Dynamic Dream:** Consider a dream with an impact so dynamic that it will last at least a hundred years after you have expired. At this stage it is only a dream; the rest of the program will give you the principles to validate and achieve its fulfillment.

4. **Develop Inspirational Dissatisfaction:** Develop an inspirational dissatisfaction with the status quo, fulfilling all the obligations of your personal responsibilities, but eagerly anticipating what is to come.

5. **Commit to the Program:** Make the commitment to move on to the next lesson, and finally complete the whole program with serious intent. Dreams have always been the catalyst of man's highest hopes and a reflection of our deepest needs.

**Note:** Be careful with whom you choose to share your dreams; dreams shared with others can be met with disbelief, humor, and ridicule.
