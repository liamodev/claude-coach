# Five Absolutes That Work

**Source:** Lesson 1 — The Dream (Destiny Directed Imageology)

Five absolutes that work when applying your imagination towards your dream.

- **Belief in Direction:** There is no point in applying your imagination to something you do not completely believe in; you severely restrict or negate what you claim you want.

- **Belief in Purpose:** You can believe you're going in the right direction because it makes good common sense.

- **Belief in Response:** Only a fool would work towards something without expecting it to do that which was specifically required. Your belief that you will get a response must be unswerving.

- **Belief in Multiplicity:** Expect and receive the add-on system of responses from your imagination; do not stop the flow for deep analysis or you may cut off the supply and limit further information. Ideas and thoughts feed and expand on one another.

- **Belief in Totality:** Do not accept half an answer; persist in believing and imagining until a full picture presents itself.

**Conclusion:** The more you combine strong belief in an idea with informed thought and consistent stimulation, the more likely the answer will appear when you least expect it. Write it down and refer to it later for add-on ideas.
