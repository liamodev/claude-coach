# A Simple Lifestyle

**Source:** Lesson 2 — The Vision

The most common cause of overwork is vagueness about where you are, where you want to go, and how you're going to get there.

## Key Steps

1. **Know your comfort zone** — Use it as a benchmark to measure progress towards discipline. Use the evaluation chart (Questions 1–8 in the lesson) to clarify your present position.

2. **Target your objectives** — Before committing to any project, ask: **What is the specific, full, and final objective?** Ensure you totally agree with it and measure it against your own desires and dreams.

3. **Time Quality Measurement** — Ask these questions of every opportunity or challenge:
   - What is the specific full and final objective?
   - Do I totally agree with it?
   - Is there a better objective?
   - Am I prepared to trade a portion of my life for it?
   - Does it offer some assurance of permanence?
   - Is it perpetual or will it have to be done again?
   - Does it fit into my plans?
   - Could it expand and thereby take more time?
   - What will happen if I don't do it?
   - Do I have total control?
   - Could I suffer an unbearable loss?

4. **Final Outcome Perspective** — Apply these questions to any project or commitment:
   - How will it affect my lifestyle?
   - Will it enhance my vision?
   - What will it cost in time, money, brains, energy, and reputation?
   - Can I get along without doing it? Or do I have an obligation?
   - Are there any limiting factors?
   - What are the direct short- and long-term benefits for me?
   - Can I afford its momentum?
   - Is the reward commensurate with the output?
   - Why am I even considering it?

5. **Delegate and align** — Does everyone know exactly what to do? Ask them to repeat it in their own words. Use the Keeping Team Alignment questions to check clarity, time frames, weak points, and progress.

6. **Expectation of yourself** — Use the daily/weekly/monthly expectation questions to take charge of your time (what you should have done, left unfinished, vacillated on, etc.).

## A Simple Lifestyle Summary

- Know your comfort zone
- Target your objectives
- Take control
- Delegate
- Maintain your expectations
