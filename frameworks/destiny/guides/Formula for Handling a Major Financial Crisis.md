# Formula for Handling a Major Financial Crisis

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Principles that can save lives. Your attitude is:
- far more relative than facts
- far more versatile than circumstances
- far more powerful than opposition
- far more resilient than pressure
- far more flexible than time
- far more valuable than money
- far more influential than positioning
- far more forceful than markets

## Fifteen Steps

1. **WRITE DOWN THE WORST KNOWN FACTS AND FEARS AND THEIR COSTS** — Taking control of your attitude; understanding timing; documenting the facts. Accept total responsibility. Think positively with a cheerful disposition.

2. **GET ALONE AND ROLE PLAY PAST VICTORIES OVER DIFFICULTIES** — Everybody has failures and difficulties. Go for a walk; clear your mind. Have a victory book—write what you have done well and read it aloud. Read biographies.

3. **PUT THE THREAT INTO PERSPECTIVE** — A threat perceived is more horrible than a threat performed. Problems are magnified under pressure. Nothing is final, not even death. Threats test your ethics. The threat you are facing is horrible—compared to what?

4. **PLAN A COUNTDOWN** — You have two choices: let it happen or make it happen. God's greatest power gift is the gift of choice. You are not a slave to attorneys, governments, or creditors. Work out a planned time frame. Retain your dignity.

5. **EXTEND THE DEADLINE** — Improve relationships; make friends with opponents. Reduce expenditure; create a survival budget. Extend time frames for solutions. Never accept that you are in a non-negotiable situation.

6. **DIVIDE AND CONQUER** — All problems are made up of component parts. Divide big problems into bite-sized chunks. Every small problem you solve reduces the whole.

7. **WORK ON A BACK-UP PLAN FOR AFTER THE EVENT** — Don't wait for the wind-down to wind-up. Think of the future while your brain juices are perking.

8. **PHONE SIX PEOPLE—ASK THEIR BIGGEST PROBLEM AND HOW THEY HANDLED IT** — Don't tell them your problem first; their story will wash away emotional debris. Then ask how they would handle yours and compile the answers.

9. **REMEMBER THE KETTLE ONLY BOILS WHEN THERE IS WATER IN IT** — The only reason you're getting pressure is because others think it will produce something. Tell them: "If you crush me I can't produce, but if you release me, I may get rich and pay you."

10. **DON'T BE SIDE-TRACKED INTO FOOLISH DECISIONS** — Be careful of schemes to make you perform by going in deeper. Keep your mind on logic. Circumstances must not force you. You are in control. Keep short reins on moral obligations.

11. **DON'T BE PRESSURED INTO UNWISE MOVES** — Do not let bank or creditors pressure you; do not sign anything under duress. Be careful of advice from well-meaning family or friends who do not fully understand. It's your life. Control it.

12. **GO ON THE ATTACK** — Work at it continually. Don't hide under the blankets. Be innovative; give people options. Keep your eye on the countdown.

13. **KEEP USING YOUR TIME** — Don't run out of puff. Create a mental second wind. Don't allow waiting time to be wasting time. Use delays for habit clean-up and disciplined assessment.

14. **KEEP SOME FUNDS IN RESERVE TO PRIME THE PUMP** — Resist throwing all money at creditors under the cloak of honesty. Dissipating every cent makes you powerless and financially sterile. Reserves allow you to continue. Without reserves, restitution can never be made.

15. **WRITE DOWN CLEARLY WHERE YOU WENT WRONG** — The second time around is easier because of the vocabulary of experiences. Lock in a formula to prevent reoccurrence. Put bad moves into reverse and produce a success system. Treat the episode as a learning experience.
