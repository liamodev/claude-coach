# Formula for Building Better People

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Ten steps to help build better people and get things done.

1. **Understand the Base Material** — You are dealing with individual human beings; they are very complex. Use a demonstration with a particular goal and a score of 1–10. People in a group are predictable; alone or under threat they are diverse and demanding. Any guess towards individual potential is usually wrong (e.g. Einstein, Churchill were considered hopeless as students). In early stages people protect their egos; they cover weaknesses by silence, talking, anger, or emotion.

2. **Remove Unwanted Baggage** — Look for and expose crutches, clichés, and family hang-ups ("We'll never make it," "You've got to get lucky," "There are no more opportunities"). Identify bad habit patterns. Do not allow wallowing in mediocrity with lack of discipline or plans. Persevere with mental blockages and lack of concentration.

3. **Create the Vision** — Use word pictures with romance. Give case history of others. Develop a mindset and write out a list of your character principles.

4. **Inspire Participants** — Awaken the giant. Introduce higher thinking. Feed the mind with inspiring diet of books, videos, and audio teachings. Surround them with those of like mind.

5. **Pragmatize the Plan** — Agree together on a mission statement. Set achievement patterns with deadlines. Give product knowledge and practical on-the-job training and know-how.

6. **Encourage and Enthuse** — Affirm your belief in people—write encouraging letters. Affirm your belief in the plan and your commitment to them. Affirm your belief in their future; remind them they can reach their dreams.

7. **Point out Small Victories** — Remind participants where they are now and how far they have progressed. Keep a checklist on their growth. Do not allow them to indulge in the security of smallness.

8. **Load on the Responsibilities** — Create situations that stretch participants. Expose their fears and program victory over them. Make difficult tasks seem easy to do.

9. **Reinforce the Vision** — Talk about and reinforce the vision by reminders. Always point to the future while developing the present. Expand the dream with new challenges.

10. **Affirm and Appreciate the Results** — Call participants periodically and tell them how proud you are. Refocus their mind on results and measurements. Start again with someone else.
