# Formula for Developing the Power of an Idea

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Seven simple principles for when you have an idea and wonder how to develop it.

1. **It Must Be Practical** — Many ideas are theoretical and never stand the test of pragmatism. Make your idea specific and measurable. Ask: Is it economically feasible? It must solve a real problem or meet a real need.

2. **It Must Have Purpose** — The idea must enrich all participants. Make sure it's not empty. It must have human and economic substance. Describe clearly what form it takes.

3. **It Must Conform To A Plan** — Is the plan to build an economic army that will encompass the whole world? How is it marketable?

4. **It Must Be Precise** — The timing must be right. Is this an idea whose time has come? Does it have a time frame? What is it?

5. **It Must Be Personalized** — Will it fulfill the individual dreams and aspirations of others? Will it be respected? Does it relate to an individual or a collective need?

6. **It Must Become Popular** — Can you utilize a multiplication factor? In other words, can it be cloned?

7. **It Must Have Permanence** — Will it be suitable for the long haul? Create a charter that is clear; make your mission statement firm. The direction must be sure. The people involved are its strength. Have you developed a track record to justify your belief in its success?
