# Six Principles to Identify Dream Stealers

**Source:** Lesson 1 — The Dream

Things or people that take your dreams away so they will never be fulfilled. Use these six principles to identify and guard against them.

1. **Lack of Focus:** Consider the dream you want realized. Can you see the full picture and verbalize it clearly? The best way to focus it is to reduce it to written form with measurements applying to each part.

2. **Fear of the Unknown:** Fear expresses itself in many forms. The most prevalent is indecisiveness; the only solution to indecisiveness is clarity. There will always be unknowns, but indecisiveness magnifies doubt and disappears once there is a firm resolve to move ahead.

3. **Admit and Learn by Mistakes:** Every mistake has its own penalty. Every mistake that is not admitted and corrected will delay your dream.

4. **Inconsistent Work Habits:** Routine and good work habits are disciplines that provide the oil to help your dream run smoothly. Break the bad habit syndrome, or you'll provide an opportunity for the dream thief to enter.

5. **Create Good Relationships:** Being at peace with all mankind is a lofty ideal. The people you do not need today may be of pivotal importance tomorrow. Broken and bad relationships build up enmity and burn up dreams and energy.

6. **Financial Security:** It becomes more difficult to dream while under financial stress unless you have unusual focus. Live within your means. If you do not save, the seeds of success are not in you.
