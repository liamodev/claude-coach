# Creating a Mission Statement: Eight Steps

**Source:** Lesson 5 — The Mission

A mission statement is a written statement of purpose and behavioral boundaries worthy of a life pursuit. Before writing, clarify: Describe Your Pain Transaction, Your Time Frame, and Your Specifics.

## The Eight Steps (Personal)

1. **The Goal** — The goal you documented in Lesson 4, fully stated so the journey can be formalized.

2. **The Vehicle** — The means that can carry the load to the goal. The vehicle must be subject to the goal. *Suggested: "The vehicle to reach my goal will always be the focus of my financial multiplication and protection by which all decisions must be tempered."*

3. **The Creed** — Personal desires that remain constant: character, beliefs, intangibles. Sustaining thrust against insurmountable odds while retaining morality. *Suggested: "I am a person of destiny… I will always maintain a positive temperament… Loyalty and commitment to family and friends… Quest for knowledge and information met by discipline in equal measure."*

4. **The Commitment** — Discipline and growth; integrity. Fulfilling the substance of every part and turning good thoughts and emotion into power. *Suggested: "My commitment to complete my mission is as sacred to me as life itself… I will make commitments carefully…"*

5. **The Qualification** — What is necessary to reach the goal. Build successfully on a small scale first. Economics, management, entrepreneurship, leadership—prove lessons by experience. *Suggested: "I will enrich my life by learning… When I cannot prove my lessons by experience, I will repeat them until I can… Truth and proof. Minimize mistakes by maximizing clarity."*

6. **The Positioning** — Place yourself at the pivotal point of progress and change. Information receiver = informed center of influence. *Suggested: "I will seek to position myself at the pivotal point… My friendships and liaisons will reflect and enhance my direction, location, mobility…"*

7. **The Assessment** — Real measurements at regular intervals. Monthly time, substance, and information checks in numerical terms. Annual major checks. *Suggested: "My mission has monthly… checks in direct relationship to progress… Major checks on an annual basis…"*

8. **The Destination** — Focus on the clarity of the final goal and the mission to achieve it; sense and enhance growth of personal qualities necessary for both. *Suggested: "Realizing that my focus must be twofold… I must also sense and enhance the growth of those personal qualities necessary for both the mission and the goal."*

When complete, sign and date your mission statement.
