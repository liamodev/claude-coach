# Leaders vs. Power Holders

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

All leaders are actual or potential power holders, but not all power holders are leaders. Use this formula to identify power holders and understand the difference.

1. Power holders arrive late and thus exercise their power to restrain.

2. Power holders use the words "I'm not sure about that" to control or withhold. The obvious thing when you're not sure is to find out or withhold comment.

3. Power holders continually ask questions but do nothing constructive or positive with the answers.

4. Power holders seek clarification on obscure or trivial matters that produce very little.

5. Power holders ask for more information when they are not using information they already have.

6. Power holders seek a pivotal point of control but accept no direct responsibility.

7. Power holders direct issues through other people to keep clear of any commitment.

8. Power holders want other people to respond directly to them, but to guard their power posture they refrain from response to others.

9. Power holders may use silence to produce mystique and even fear.

10. Power holders may befriend you to gain intimate information that allows them to gain influence over your thoughts and directions.

11. Power holders often defend or provoke action or thought on a principle without any real personal commitment to such a principle.
