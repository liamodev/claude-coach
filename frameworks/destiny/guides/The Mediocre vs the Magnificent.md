# The Mediocre vs. The Magnificent

**Source:** Lesson 4 — The Goal

Because man is a goal-striving being, it is necessary to distinguish between the mediocre and the magnificent. In destiny goals we look far beyond the ordinary to the extraordinary and exceptional because our minds are fixed on world changes. We are prepared to risk our lives in the pursuit.

## Five Reasons for Destiny-Level Goals

1. **The overwhelming evidence** that the world has progressed because of champions who dared to risk under the spell of a mighty vision.

2. **The recognition** that we need extraordinary goals to expose our limitless capacities and focus our boundless energies.

3. **The awareness** that without a destiny goal, our lives will account for very little and our souls will feel barren and unfulfilled.

4. **The belief** that we have a responsibility towards others to act as part of the engine that drives spaceship Earth.

5. **The realization** that the gift of life is not for selfish indulgence, but is an opportunity to add to what others have done before us.

Any major goal requires the involvement of people. If you need money, influence, assistance, power, protection, or almost anything else—it must come from people. The backdrop of your destiny goal must be viewed with that in mind.
