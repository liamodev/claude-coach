# Control Your Time

**Source:** Lesson 2 — The Vision

*Your life is a time zone that is exclusively yours. Control it!*

## What Is Your Life Worth?

Work through the valuation exercise:
- How much longer do you expect to have an effective working life?
- What would you be valued at for physical labor? For mental strategy?
- What price would you put on your soul, your dreams, your experience and heritage?
- **Would you sell your life for that figure?** The only person who values you at that figure is you.

You alone own your lifespan. It can be controlled by you, wasted, given away, sold, or used for growth and enjoyment.

## Practical Steps

1. **Plan undisturbed time** — Find a quiet spot away from distractions; set aside time for your most demanding and important tasks. Concentrated time of freedom will multiply what you get done.

2. **Evaluate your position** — Each morning or at the start of each week, spell out what must be done and set time aside to complete each item. Arrange everything else around these requirements.

3. **Learn to say no** — Say no to irritating items and requests that squeeze out the things that must be done. No compromise. Make it a contest of your will against circumstances.

4. **Make the phone subject to your convenience** — If you don't, it will control you. Discourage interruptions to the programs you have committed yourself to.

## Attitude Check

- Do you feel guilty when you do not accept a phone call or visitors? Should you?
- When you isolate yourself for concentrated work, do you get more done? Ought you to do it more often?
- How much more effective would you be if you discouraged interruptions?
- List six ways you are prevented from getting more done—and give a possible solution and action.

## Summary

- What is your life worth?
- Plan undisturbed time
- Control contact and technology
