# The Goals Formula

**Source:** Lesson 4 — The Goal

Six steps for setting destiny goals. The mission statement is the linchpin—it provides the sustaining thrust to get the job done.

1. **Define Your Goal.** It must be measurable and specific. If you do not already have a goal, then make finding a goal your goal.

2. **Set Out Your Strategy.** If you cannot measure it, you cannot manage it. (This is your mission statement.)

3. **Plan To Deal with Your Problems.** This covers all problems; dealing with them must also become part of your mission statement. Problems always beg solutions, and solutions always create growth.

4. **Build in Reserves.** Reserves must be built into the ultimate goal to prevent collapse and into the mission statement as a protection against losing any ground already gained.

5. **Relate to Time Frames.** Time frames need to be assessed against the ultimate goal and locked in for crucial staging points of the mission journey.

6. **Create a Master Plan.** In creating a master plan for goal setting with all its ramifications, the linchpin must be the mission statement because it provides a sustaining thrust to get the job done.
