# Clarifying Your Goals: Four Areas

**Source:** Lesson 4 — The Goal

The important requirement is to know what your ultimate goal is—without a goal your target is vague. Goals are divided into four main areas:

## 1. Identity Goals

Dealing with who we are, what we stand for, and our personality development. Four distinct areas:
- **Character** — spiritual/religious convictions, attitude to money, ethics, politics, thought patterns, faith
- **Relationships** — how you love and treat family, friends, relatives; what you want them to think of you
- **Ambition** — capacity to perform; develop the ability to act and perform
- **Economics** — financial control, stable foundation; wealth is a thought—pattern of thinking, good priority, decision making, disciplined action

**ABC Test for Identity Goals:** (A) Specify special identity dynamics needed. (B) Put a % against each (total 100%). (C) Put a % indicating where you are now vs. ultimate requirements.

## 2. Functional Goals

Physical well-being to function effectively, provide for wants and needs, assure comfort and security. Three areas:
- **Health** — diet, exercise, stress control, rest; goals in these areas throughout life
- **Education** — focus on objective and pivotal points of your destiny goal; information must be stored before it can be used beneficially
- **Mental stability** — ability to handle criticism; see the big picture; control emotions

**ABC Test for Functional Goals:** Same structure—specify dynamics, % for achievement, % for current position.

## 3. Structural Goals

The means by which you reach your destiny goals. Three primary areas:
- **Platform** — recognition, respectability, provision for extensions and continued usability
- **Support** — supportive goals, junctions reached by certain times with certain dynamics in place
- **Reserves** — finance, time, energy, mental and spiritual reserves to prevent total collapse

**ABC Test for Structural Goals:** Same structure—imperative structural dynamics, % for goal, % for current position.

## 4. Destiny Goals

When setting a destiny goal, the normal must be ignored, the ordinary discarded, the routine feared. Destiny goals are changes, discoveries, or events that stand tall against the backdrop of history and aim for great impact on human need.

**Helps for identifying your destiny goal:**
- Must result from your own commitment, involvement, and responsibility
- Must create change, make something new, solve a problem, or prevent catastrophe on a world scale with permanence
- Unattempted or unfulfilled must leave a vacuum
- Must be in accord with and measured against your destiny support system
- Must have room for expansion and improvement with add-on possibilities

**Make sure your goal has substance.** Ask: How is that going to change the world?
