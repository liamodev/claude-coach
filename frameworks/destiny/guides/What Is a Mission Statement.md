# What Is a Mission Statement

**Source:** Lesson 5 — The Mission

- A mission statement can be described as a **written statement of purpose and behavioral boundaries** worthy of a life pursuit.

- A mission statement **sets a pattern of behavior** that exhibits the character of an organization or person.

- A mission statement is a **style of pursuit**.

- A mission statement **provides the ethos** for an organization or a person.

- A mission statement **reflects the spirit rather than the law** of a person or organization.

- A mission statement **creates the relationship culture** of a person or an organization.

- A mission statement is a **quasi trustee** of a person or an organization.

- A mission statement is a **secure anchor** in the stormy seas of life.

If you need to try and adjust your mission statement to suit a particular set of circumstances, this can only mean: (1) The mission statement was not thoroughly thought out in the first place, (2) Your value system has changed, or (3) The destination is still not clear.

A mission statement that is ambiguous, sloppy, and open to manipulation always provides a way for the unscrupulous to enter and the uncommitted to exit. Beware of long explanations and justifications for changing, manipulating, or overriding a mission statement.
