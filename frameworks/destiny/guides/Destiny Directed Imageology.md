# Destiny Directed Imageology

**Source:** Lesson 1 — The Dream

Creativity that is uniquely yours. Everyone has a creative capacity that often remains partly dormant; there are no dull minds, only dormant minds.

- Disciplined Thought Level

## Levels of Imageology (Illustration B)

From lowest to highest:

1. **Free-Rein Erratic Response** — Totally out-of-control imagination.
2. **Hereditary, Imposed Response** — Mental faculties with hereditary components; can be changed with discipline.
3. **Relative Circumstantial Response** — Behavior changes with circumstances and company.
4. **Emotional, Relative Response** — Imagination responds to joy, anger, bitterness, or sadness.
5. **Automatic Experiential Response** — Experiences stored in the mind act as catalyst; ask "What is the lesson here?"
6. **Principle-Controlled Response** — Principles govern life; imagination follows what the person truly believes.
7. **Pre-Determined Stimulant Response** — Predetermine what your imagination will do (see Illustration C).
8. **Automatic Selective Flow** — Sophisticated search without stimuli; insights and ideas as common occurrence.
9. **Flash Imageology** — Extraordinary flash of information; genius-level insights.

## Illustration C: Predetermined Stimulant

- A problem or opportunity to focus on.
- An idea of what we would like the results to be.
- An honest openness to all input, whether directly relevant or not.
- A sincere belief that an answer exists and will come.

**Practice:** Write down your thoughts in sequence. Persevere by writing even idle thoughts; in time the imagination will flow. Encourage flash imageology with focus, concentrated thought, an informed mind, accepting all ideas before judgment, and writing thoughts down so they don't escape.
