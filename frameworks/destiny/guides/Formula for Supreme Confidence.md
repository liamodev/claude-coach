# Formula for Supreme Confidence

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Timidity is often a physical posture and a visible behavioral pattern. Fear kills confidence.

## Fears That Kill Confidence

Fear of failure, embarrassment, rejection, being wrong, exposure, success, not knowing, unpreparedness, others, consequences, not looking right, saying the wrong thing.

## Sixteen Principles for Supreme Confidence

1. **Courage Creates Confidence** — Treat failures as lessons; embarrassments as someone else's problem; rejection as temporary; mistakes recognized create good judgment; exposure leads to truth; success is enjoyable; lack of knowledge creates appetite to learn; others also fear you; nothing can hurt you.

2. **Overcome Fear** — Command attention with eye contact. Use a voice tone that is deep and sincere. Over prepare and you will always have confidence.

3. **Get Cooperation** — Ask for help in areas of doubt. Select competent people with proven ability. Investigate how others have actually done it.

4. **Present Yourself Like a Winner** — Become involved. Under commit and over perform. Dress up, not down.

5. **Ask the Right Questions** — Talk to others about themselves. What is their mission in life? Create let-out phrases for embarrassing moments.

6. **Create Opportunities** — Think of unusual practical ideas. Relate to the unrelatable. Give several options.

7. **Say What People Like to Hear** — Be complimentary and encouraging. Tell them you appreciate their time. Refer to their families or favorite projects.

8. **Plan the Process** — Before any situation, prepare. Have prepared conversational topics and questions. Learn about their recreation preferences.

9. **Use Affirmations** — Show them something that works. Refer to mutual friends or admirers. Use self-talk before appointments.

10. **Rehearse Thoroughly** — Write out a script with key points. Practice on a friend or colleague. Change only when repetitive mistakes prove it wrong.

11. **Ignore Setbacks and Learn Persistence** — Use failure as a lesson. Never give up. Document what went wrong.

12. **Reprogram Your Past** — Dismiss bad memories; start life afresh today. Use yesterday's successes for today's inspiration. Bad experiences are stretching points to overcome.

13. **Demystify the Ego** — A strong ego is not wrong; an ego out of control or a weak ego is wrong. Volunteering your achievement to a limited degree is reasonable if truthful. Don't let anyone talk you down.

14. **You Are the Product** — Walk, sit, stand, sound, phrase correctly; command correctly. Keep car inside and outside clean. Use deodorants; do not take liberties without invitation.

15. **Make Yourself Necessary—Become a Pivotal Point** — Always look for the critical path. Make yourself indispensable. Do what you do better than anyone else.

16. **Learn Useful Etiquette** — There is no right way to do the wrong thing. Stand when someone enters room. Learn correct table and social graces.
