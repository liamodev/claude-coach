# Four Questions to Measure Your Progress

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

Four questions to measure your progress on the path of greatness.

1. **What age have you set yourself to reach your full potential that God might maximize your life?**  
   Forget limitations, ignore time restraints. Come to grips with the gift called life and make a decision. The imagination is more powerful than the will. Set a target; God has given you the authority through free will. Do not let others influence you—you must take personal responsibility.

2. **Can you tell me in 50 pages or more what your full potential is in every area of your life?**  
   Write a resume of who you are and hidden talents in conversational form. Divide life into sections: spiritual, physical, mental, family, finance. Crystallize your greatest potential in every area. Be honest and positive. Write it in a lined book and date it. Read it often; only make amendments after great consideration.

3. **Accepting your full potential as 100%, what percentage rating would you give yourself right now?**  
   Approach as preparing for a long journey. List tools and supplies; note what you already have (assets). Put a percentage on personal equipment you have and lack against 100%. Even 5% is a launching pad. Be results-oriented; the scoreboard must be real. Professional qualifications are a starting point, not the final goal.

4. **Accepting the deficiency between your two scores, what plans are you going to make to take up the shortfall and when?**  
   Use the difference as a motivator for calculated action. Plans must be personal. Estimate lifespan (exclude accidents, include hereditary background and habits). Set life goals within that time frame. A magnificent obsession to achieve can extend life ten years or more. If you do not plan for life, you will do things according to circumstance instead of planned consent.
