# Apply Strong Pressure to Specific Tasks

**Source:** Lesson 2 — The Vision

*Evaluate and concentrate on your final destination.*

To take charge of your time, carefully choose items on your agenda that can be accelerated. Be courageous and creative enough to avoid "the cowardice of conformity." Every process in life has waiting time, obsolete functions, errors, margins, and room for improvement.

## Questions When Commencing a Project

- Am I on the right track?
- Is there a better way to do this?
- Am I going straight to the problem areas, or creating a process that goes around the problem?
- Am I moving at the right speed?
- Sometimes spending money can save you time—don't be timid about offering cash to get the job done.
- Am I using enough force? Remind contractors that a continued relationship depends on your satisfaction. Remind staff of the commitment and skill required for continued employment.

## Practical Steps

- Go to work very early or stay late for one or two weeks; set tight time frames for specific work to be completed.
- **Beware:** Do not always be a pressure cooker to yourself or others, or you'll burst the pipes and get less done. Create a friendly, competitive atmosphere instead.

## Creating Your Own Life-Character

1. Why do I want more time?
2. How much time do I want encumbered?
3. How can I create more free time?
4. What can I do now to avoid wasting time?
5. Write down three ways you've wasted major time throughout your life.

**Next step:** Turn problems into procedures. Build character traits that encourage doing things right the first time to prevent being robbed of time by avoidable repetition.

Time is irredeemable and limited. What you do with it and the respect you give it can expand your capacity for work and enjoyment beyond any imaginary dream.
