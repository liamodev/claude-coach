# Special Distinctives of a Leader

**Source:** Lesson 6 — The Destiny

1. A leader does not get distracted away from the main cause—issues that use time and energy out of proportion to the dominant cause.

2. A leader is prepared to accept immediate deprivation in exchange for future gratification.

3. A leader has a realistic perspective on the urgent.

4. A leader is prepared to accept failures with insight and optimism.

5. Leaders attain, that they might use that attainment to expand and share growth with others.

6. Leaders very often move from crisis to crisis, losing some and winning others, but always gaining ground at every opportunity.

## Three Major Principles for Leadership Development

1. Your realistic understanding of your position and capacity.

2. How much of yourself you are prepared to give.

3. The size of your dreams.
