# Discovering and Defining Your Destiny Goal

**Source:** Lesson 4 — The Goal

## Discovering Your Destiny Goal

1. Write down 10 ways in which you would like to leave your mark on the world. Imagine you have no limitations.

2. Place these items in order of importance to the world using a rating system of 1 to 10.

3. Against each item indicate the degree to which each would:
   - give you satisfaction
   - benefit the world
   - have a lasting effect
   - be possible

Do this after unhurried reflection and careful examination using the goal formula.

## Defining My Goal

**Describe the following:**
- My destiny goal is …
- It will change the world because …
- I must personally do it because …

**My supportive goals are:** identity goals, functional goals, structural goals

## My Strategy For My Goals

- Personal qualifications required
- Information needed
- Positions to be attained
- Acquisitions to be realized

## Setting Out My Problems and Their Solutions

On careful examination of my goals, I foresee the following difficulties and have made the following plans towards a solution. (Respond to each area identified as problem and solution.)

## Building Reserves

To ensure I have sufficient reserves to withstand a sudden crisis or a prolonged difficult period, I commit myself to the following:
- I will create special friends by …
- I will strengthen my body by …
- I will develop my spiritual faith by …
- I will secure my assets by …

## Relating Goals to Time Frames

- **Short-term** (up to 3 years): completion dates
- **Medium-term** (4–9 years): completion dates
- **Long-term** (10 years or more): completion dates

Recognizing that a goal needs finality, I plan to achieve my [short/medium/long]-term destiny goals within …… years, at which time I will be …… years of age.
