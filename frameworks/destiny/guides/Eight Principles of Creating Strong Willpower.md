# Eight Principles of Creating Strong Willpower

**Source:** Lesson 3 — The Commitment

To fulfill your commitment will take enormous willpower. When the heat is on and others turn their backs on you, these eight principles have always brought me through. God is committed to your development; the will underlies the integrity of the soul.

1. **Understand the cost–benefit ratio**
   - A strong will develops character; a weak will is the enemy to character.
   - A strong will creates self-esteem; a weak will gives shame.
   - A strong will builds relationship; a weak will destroys trust.
   - A strong will helps; a weak will hurts.

2. **Remove the mystique.** A strong will is natural, not supernatural. It can be clearly understood. If you make a commitment to yourself, you must develop the integrity to keep it.

3. **Clarify your values.** What are the most important things in life to you? Put a value measurement on each one.

4. **Open your mind to a new thought.** Have an intellectual stretch occasionally and put your life up for scrutiny.

5. **Make the commencement easy.** Take time out to be alone and dream. Write a short biography of what you would like your life to be. Write down the names of all your friends and alongside, how you would like them to describe you.

6. **Begin the formula.** Develop clarity of thought and an uncluttered lifestyle. Know what track you want to run on.

7. **Developing the formula.** For six weeks only, commit yourself only to what you will finish.

8. **Owning the formula.** Know your life's values and document what you want to do with them by building an acceptable plan.
