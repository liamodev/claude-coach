# Choose Words Carefully and Accurately to Save Time

**Source:** Lesson 2 — The Vision

*Get into the habit of saying things clearly once.*

A great deal of unnecessary work is caused by resolving misunderstandings and clarifying instructions or comments. Poor communication can lead to argument, suspicion, and polarization—all of which waste time.

## For Specific Results

1. Exactly what do I want to achieve by my statement?
2. How will the recipient react?
3. Clarify in writing, if necessary, exactly what you want to say.
4. Practice using the correct tone for what you need to say.

Use simple words with uncluttered meanings: e.g. "no" rather than "I don't think so," or "3:30 PM" rather than "mid afternoon." Be specific, clear, and avoid misunderstanding. Do your thinking before you're talking. Consider putting commitments, instructions, and interpretations in writing.

Save time by getting others used to the fact that you only speak on a subject once with clarity and conviction—then action commences without further signals. Your communication time will be halved and productivity will skyrocket.

## Confronting Others: Role and Identity Method

Separate the person from the role. Example: "Jim, you are a very likable person with many talents. But there is another side—your role in this organization. Your role is to manage our books, and in that area your work has not measured up to the standards we have set." You're not attacking the person but centering on the role.

## Reliable Associates

Life is usually choices, not chances. Look for: Do they keep their word? Do they have a good reputation? Are they skilled in their area? What is their success/failure ratio? Have their predictions been accurate? Avoid those who exaggerate or blame circumstances, others, machinery, and markets. Beware of "rainbow seekers" with new schemes. Determine the limits of tolerable behavior and keep control of your time.

## Summary

- Give clear, specific direction
- Confront problems promptly
- Think before you speak
- Say it once
- Look for reliable associates
