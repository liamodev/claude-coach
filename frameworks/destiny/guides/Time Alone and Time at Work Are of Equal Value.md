# Time Alone and Time at Work Are of Equal Value

**Source:** Lesson 2 — The Vision

My most important work gets done when I am alone. As I reflect, imagine, and plan, my thought process is stimulated and I produce.

- **Wood cutter** — needs to stop to sharpen the axe.
- **Pastor** — needs to revitalize the spirit.
- **Athlete** — needs to relax and rest the body.
- **Soldier** — rest and recreation are imperative for balance and resilience.

The leader may not need the same type or quality of solitude as a follower, but respites are essential: a couple of days at a country property, time at the library to think and reflect, or an extended weekend for determined, concentrated, and creative thought.

*"He who refreshes others, he himself will be refreshed."* — Proverbs 11:25

Be careful to treat all commitments as moral obligations. Do not commit yourself to anything you do not want to do. Put a measurement of time and value on everything. **Money is only time well spent.** If you have enough time, you can always earn more money—but money cannot buy your personal time.
