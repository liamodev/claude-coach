**The dedication to think, plan, meditate, and learn.**

Philippians 3:14 ' I pressed towards the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Jesus Christ'

Whenever the subject of goals is presented, we tend to identify the final objective required. Then we hold that picture before our eyes mentally so that we neither wander nor waver as we visualize its accomplishment. This gives us a sense of ownership of the goal. There is a better way.

There is a better way. In setting destiny goals, one has to take a more panoramic view at first and solely examine what will be needed to attain the goal. It will take insight to identify what is required. Then you must marshal your forces into line, ready for mobilization towards a predetermined destination. 

Next time you are in the center of a big city, stop for a while at a busy intersection and observe the crowds as they pass by. As you look into their faces, acknowledge that it is through people such as these that goals are accomplished. 

Any major goal requires the involvement of people. Although they are sometimes not seen, they are certainly there behind the scenes, doing the ordinary and extraordinary things that form a pattern of support or resistance.
  
Response from others is an essential ingredient to all goal setting, but especially in the more advanced and experienced science of destiny goals. 

If you need money, influence, assistance, power, protection, protection, or almost anything else for that matter, then it must come from people. So the backdrop of your destiny goal must be viewed with that in mind.
  
### The Mediocre vs. The Magnificent

Because man is a goal striving being, it is necessary to distinguish between the mediocre and the magnificent. Most people set goals that are only of the standard of the average human being, with perhaps some element of stretch built in that adds spice to the chase. In destiny goals, we are looking far beyond the ordinary to the extraordinary and the exceptional because our minds are fixed on world changes. We are prepared to risk our lives in the pursuit of these. 

There are several reasons for this.

1. The overwhelming evidence that the world has progressed because of champions who dared to risk under the spell of a mighty vision.

2. The recognition that we need extraordinary goals to expose our limitless capacities and focus our boundless energies.

3. The awareness that without a destiny goal, our lives will account for very little and our souls will feel barren and unfulfilled.

4. The belief that we have a responsibility towards others to act as it were as part of the engine that drives spaceship Earth.

5. The realization that the gift of life is not for selfish indulgence, but is an opportunity to add to what others have done before us.


### The Goals Formula

1. **Define Your Goal.** It must be measurable and specific. If you do not already have a goal, then make finding a goal your goal.

2. **Set Out Your Strategy.** If you cannot measure it, then, of course, you cannot manage it. (Here we are really talking about your mission statement.)

3. **Plan To Deal with Your Problems.** This covers all problems, and dealing with them must also become part of your mission statement. Problems always beg solutions, and solutions always create growth.

4. **Build in Reserves.** Reserves must be built into the ultimate goal to prevent collapse and into the mission statement as a protection against losing any ground that has already been gained.

5. **Relate to Time Frames.** Just as time-frame need to be assessed against the ultimate goal, so must time frames be locked in for crucial staging points of the mission journey.

6. **Create a Master Plan.** In creating a master plan for goal setting with all its ramifications, the linchpin must be the mission statement because it provides a sustaining thrust to get the job done.

### Clarifying Your Goals

The important requirement here is to know what your ultimate goal is because without a goal your target is vague. 

To clarify your thoughts and to create a system of order to enable you to understand more completely the goal setting process, I have divided this lesson into four main areas:

1. **Identity Goals** - dealing with who we are, what we stand for, and our personality development.

2. **Functional Goals** - relating to all the things that need to be done to create a stable life.

3. **Structural Goals** - providing the superstructure to undergird long-term high achievement.

4. **Destiny Goals** - seeing our life's work on a world scale and the legacy and the impact it produces.

### 1. Identity Goals

Who you are and what your plans are for development of your character form part of your overall destiny goals. 

Identity goals can be summarized into four very distinct areas:
- Character
- Relationships
- Ambition
- Economics

#### Character
Understanding your character will help you clarify and sharpen your sense of who you really are and what you hope to be. Character includes many things, but in essence, these can be regarded as spiritual or religious convictions will which will create and determine attitude to money, ethics, politics, thought patterns, and faith regarding one's creation, existence, and the hereafter. 

Commitment to a belief in a character standard for life that embodies absolutes and benevolent and disciplined attitudes and which combines the element of truth and faith does much to build a life that is respected by others and beneficial to the participant and the community as a whole. Absolutes tend to give balance and perspective in difficult situations, reflect a permanence which provides a backdrop for future events and provides balance and stability for daily living. 

Those people who build a life around Christian absolutes can adopt a futuristic perspective because they are confident in their boundaries that they have set for themselves and are secure in a vacillating world. A lack of confidence comes when uncertainty prevails and stability is threatened. Confidence can be restored when a sound foundation is firmly established and belief is strong. 

Affirming a commitment to your belief may be the easiest side of the equation, whereas living out that belief irrespective of circumstances is quite different because after all, it's said and done. Far more is said than done.

Many put this area of life into the too hard basket or adopt some laissez faire attitude without realizing that it could be the most important of all life's tasks. Why not set a goal to honestly investigate the claims of Jesus Christ? Encyclopedia Britannica develops a thousands of words to this person Jesus, more words than it gives to Aristotle, Alexander, Cicero, Julius Caesar, or Napoleon Bonaparte. 

Wouldn't it be sad to discover too late in life that power, confidence, and security could have been yours, but you ignored this great gift, relationships.

#### Relationships

Your identity can be recognized by the way you love and treat your family, friends, relatives, and the people who are important to you. Too often, we leave this very important area of life to chance and impulse, not realizing that there are enormous rewards for the serious destiny goal seeker in those areas. 

What do you really want this group of people to think of you? How do you want them to respond to you? What goals do you believe you can create to make these relationship more meaningful and more secure? What are the special dynamics needed to enhance the richness of the relationship? These and other powerful questions must be answered and acted upon to form and secure the foundations of your destiny goals.

In my extensive studies of the lies of outstanding achievers, it seems that many of them reached a point where what was possible could have been passed by. Some retired or went into seclusion, only to come back later at a seemingly more opportune time, full of vigor and ready to adventure once again. It certainly seemed to me that many of the greats reached a point in their lives where they ran out of puff. In other words, the pursuit of the vision became stale. I'm reminded of the words spoken by the great sir Winston Churchill just before he when he said, I am so bored with it all.

#### Ambition

All your dreams, goals, plans, and drive will amount to nothing unless you develop the capacity to perform what is required. Often failure is a reflection of the way the performance or reaction has been misdirected or underdeveloped rather than the initiator's lack of capacity. Whether you are in business or any other endeavor, make sure that you continually inquire and make doubly sure that the person you are dealing with has the power to act as well as the capacity to perform. 

The best advice that I could give anyone who wants to achieve in any field is to learn to sell without salary or retainer and subsist only on commission. Rarely do I find those who are prepared to test themselves against such unforgiving standards to realize their real level of confidence.

Why not try as a test to put your life up for scrutiny in the area of your choice, testing as it were, your capacity against all odds. 

Do not contrast or evaluate your performance on the sporting field or use academic, spiritual, or military information in substitution for business, as these cannot be cross pollinated or replaced for field experience and will only create a doubtful result. Consider also your intelligence absorption rate for quickly assessing on the job difficulties, and always be alert to the source of trouble.

#### Economics 

Most of us have an opinion about the rich and tend to admire, envy, or criticize those in our community who have wealth. We conveniently forget that if we are Christians, our role is not to philosophize and debate about those who are rich, but rather to reach down and lift up those who are unfortunate enough to be poor. 

Money is simply minted personality. What it can do for an individual or group must be understood on the basis that it is a neutral commodity until it is put in service. 

The battle for financial control of your life and a stable, secure foundation of economics will probably cause you more concern, frustration, anguish, and work than any other pursuit in your life.

Inconsistent and unmanageable financial behavior and control have been proven to cause both physical and mental illness, divorce, broken relationships, suicide, legal battles, violence of all kinds, and depression. The lack of money causes malnutrition even in Western democracies, particularly as our inability to care for the aged, the sick, and the young escalates. Yet some people say they have no interest in wealth and that money is as an important item in their lives. The fact remains that a little more for some can really make the difference between living and dying. 

The opportunity to earn is more of a pursuit than a gift. When the quest for responsible financial security is abandoned, what usually follows is apathy, stagnation of the mind, grubbiness, and laziness in personal behavior and hygiene, The result of abandonment of personal responsibility is the abandonment of hope and the placing of blame on someone else or a system or a series of events. Personal responsibility and the acceptance of one's action or inaction irrespective of circumstances is the first step towards a healthy mind and a hopeful future. 

**Wealth is a thought...** Some suggest that wealth is obtained by extensive education, but that is not borne out by those who obtained and possess great wealth. While it is necessary to obtain a sound basic education, it only requires the most cursory investigation to observe that even business schools are unable to produce entrepreneurs, and that the best business trainers, when faced with redundancy themselves, usually fail to make grade in the changing world of business. 

Financial growth and stability has more to do with the pattern of thinking, good priority, decision making, and disciplined action than with circumstances or social positioning. 

To obtain wealth requires a special mindset that releases the imagination and drives the will. The more you tend to insulate yourself from risk, the more you tend to insulate yourself from wealth. 

Wealth seekers are always on the offensive and rarely on the defensive, which requires a continued vigilant alertness to opportunity awareness of impending danger.

### 2. Functional Goals

You will not reach destiny goals unless you also plan for your physical well-being to enable you to function effectively, provide for the wants and needs of life, and assure some level of comfort and security. 

Functional goals generally fall into three areas: 
- Health
- Education
- Mental stability

#### Health 

To lead a full life requires energy. The more knowledge we have with respect to the benefit of diet, exercise, and attitude, the more our opportunity for a productive life can be expanded. To consider a destiny goal and yet ignore the vital area of health could severely limit the possibility of achievement. 

It is almost impossible in today's fast world where we travel extensively to jump time zones and to cope with strange customs and food without feeling physically stressed. That is why some simple but firm goals need to be established and adhered to throughout life in the areas of diet, exercise, stress control, and rest. 

Many people fail because they are fatigued and unable to get the best out of their creative energies or to sustain physical endurance. We need to look after our bodies, realizing that in doing so, we are going to be able to extend ourselves to do much more, to go that one step further, which is a touchstone of a champion.

#### Education

It is generally thought that our main education is gained at schools, colleges, and universities, but the facts present a different story. When it comes to the super achiever or the entrepreneur, schools, colleges, and universities are an important and functional part of our education and provide a basis for information and learning. But the truly productive education comes sometime after that as we learn and become involved in the cut and thrust of life and experience and explore the possibilities that surround us. 

The key is to concentrate with intensity on that which you have accepted as your destiny in life and relate to those things that can influence its path. Focus your education on the objective and pivotal points of your destiny goal, remembering at all times that information must be conveniently stored before it can be used beneficially towards a predetermined goal.

#### Mental Stability

The ability to handle criticism is high on the list of success characteristics. 

The crushing, hurtful, often wrongful remarks made by others tend to deflect people from their course and bog them down with mental tension. 

Try to bear in mind criticism on its own has no power over you unless you give it voluntarily. While you are tossing and turning a bed trying to assess what that person meant by what they said or where the information came from, you are giving criticism the power drive to destroy your life. 

Mark Twain once said, 'If there's any real power in criticism, the skunk would have been extinct years ago'.

#### The Ability To See The Big Picture

To see the big picture requires courage against the odds and being prepared to have confidence in your own judgment. 

A person of destiny needs the patience to put all the pieces together and develop a panoramic view of the possibilities with measurements, specifics, and time frames. 

When viewing the big picture, destiny people see very clearly their pivotal role in the equation and the clear results that will come.

#### The Ability To Control Your Emotions

Controlling one's emotion must not be confused with coldness. Emotion under control indicates a strong ego, but an ego out of control or an ego needing to be propped up continually is a weak ego. 

The wrong word in anger can hang around like a forty year mortgage, and wrong action to provoke anger, hurt, or defiance can have a devastating effect on your reputation and create a buildup of enmity from others.

### 3. Structural Goals

You have reflected on developing your identity and planning your physical well-being. Now you need to look at the means by which you are going to reach your destiny goals. 

Sound structural goals ensure that you are not left vulnerable as you reach for your ultimate goals and create new opportunities. 

Structural goals can be divided into three primary areas: 
- Platform
- Support
- Reserves

#### Platform

Without a platform, you can be lost in a cacophony of voices seeking an opportunity to be heard. While it is true that everyone has a right to speak, very few have earned or obtained the opportunity to be heard.

A person who has gained recognition, whether it be by achievement in sports, music, politics, religion, or even business, finds it much easier to get things done, for the simple reason that the media and those with power and influence usually are more inclined to give attention to what that person says. 

The stronger the platform, the more attention you get. The curious thing is that it does not always have to be related to power or money. A sports person or a humble person such as Mother Teresa can claim the attention of the whole world because of their special circumstances. Make developing your own platform a goal, and make sure it has respectability with provision for extensions and continued usability.

#### Support

Your destiny goal needs supportive goals that are part of the matrix of the whole plan. Certain junctions have to be reached by certain times with certain dynamics in place in order for the final thrust toward the destiny goal to be achieved. Your goals will probably include minor and major goals, long and short term in duration that follow a pattern of reaching point A before proceeding to point B, and so on. All of these support goals are part of the superstructure and hold the whole of the plan together. 

A destiny goal remains elusive if there is no plan. The words of Shakespeare appear so true, where he says some men are born great, some achieve greatness, and some have greatness thrust upon them. 

The evidence is overwhelming with regard to those who have had greatness thrust upon them and those who were born with greatness. But the field is wide open for those who would dare to seek to achieve greatness through serious plans.

#### Reserves

A cautious note needs to be sounded again and again. Don't forget reserves. Having backup plan does not indicate a lack of vision or faith. It is simply a sensible course to follow. Any expedition going into the unknown will carry reserves for emergency. 

Finance, time, energy, and mental and spiritual reserves prevent total collapse or the aborting of something that is attempted during emergency or crisis. 

What a pity it would be after having strategized, worked, and planned for years. All is lost because of the unexpected circumstances that took you by surprise and stole your dream. 

Make sure that your financial reserves are beyond the reach of the unscrupulous financial scalpers and bounty hunters who seek to plunder all those with whom they come in contact. Many past rules for survival and economics are no longer valid because the world has shrunk due to the advances in transport, technology, and communication. 

Be wise, and plan reserves to protect the ground that you have already gained.

### 4. Destiny Goals

When setting a destiny goal, the normal must be ignored, the ordinary must be discarded, and the routine must be feared. Destiny goals are those changes, discoveries, or events that stand tall against the backdrop of history and aim for such a great and awesome impact on human need that their presence is felt on the stage of international affairs. 

Achievement need not be entirely attributed to the individual involved, as there can be significant external circumstances which contribute. 

Timing, good and bad breaks, special events and circumstances may seem to be the predominant reason for achievement, but it is you who must be the pivotal point of the whole scheme. Whether you are in the shadows or are thrust into the limelight, your destiny is for you to attain and for others to observe.

Using the previous lessons on imageology to widen your perspective in every direction, consider the following 'helps' towards identifying your destiny goal.

1. A destiny goal must result from your own personal commitment, involvement, and responsibility.

2. A destiny goal must create a change, make something new, solve a problem, or prevent a catastrophe on a world scale with some permanence.

3. A destiny goal unattempted or unfulfilled must leave a vacuum.

4. A destiny goal must always be in accord with and measured against your destiny support system.

5. A destiny goal must always have room for expansion and improvement with add on possibilities for further impact.
