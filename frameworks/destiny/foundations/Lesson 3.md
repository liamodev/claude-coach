# Lesson Three: The Commitment
  
**The Acceptance of Pain as a Cost Towards a Benefit.**

**Biblical Base for Commitment:**

2 Timothy 2:2-3 'And these things that you have heard from me among many witnesses, commit these to faithful men who will be able to teach others also. You therefore must endure hardship as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.'

The standard for a healthy mind is the automatic acceptance of responsibility for one's own actions and choices and the results that come from them.

The word commitment immediately conjures up in the mind a fear of a loss, subserviency or pain. For reasons hidden in the human psyche, the benefit of commitment has to loom large or be of such minimal consequence that there is little or no penalty. 

The lives of many are wasted by avoiding exaggerating or fearfully and timidly moving in and out of commitments to the point that the very thought of a commitment for life raises such strong protection barriers that the decision to make a commitment is lost in the oblivion of vagueness, uncertainty, and distrust.
  
A life is given to use, to be spent. God does not robotize a human mind to prevent or force a commitment. 

At the end of our sojourn here on this planet, we will be able to look back at opportunities lost or grasped according to our own willingness to make commitments. 

The fact that you have come thus far in this program called destiny of the third millennium indicates your desire for a life commitment towards a predetermined destiny of your own creation. 

Now is the time for the great transaction of your life, for a mighty purpose, a collision of magnitude. You may put it off, but you have come this far, and to move any further requires a deliberate dynamic until death commitment.
  
In the latter years of my life I've met old friends and acquaintances of many years ago who are still clinging to their past philosophies of life and abound by untested but good sounding cliches. 

At this point comes a major separation and only a few will pass this test of the will with resolute action, character, and wisdom.
  

### The Threshold of Performance

The dream that you developed into a vision must now pass through the commitment threshold. Tenacity and perception in partnership with discipline and effort will be essential to produce the end results. Whatever happens do not get into the theory trap creating great plans and developing good strategies in lieu of personal exposure to the pain of performance.

It is at this point I should mention that pain is weakness escaping. Strength and direction will replace your anxieties and reluctance finally with results. 

Choose several areas of actual direct personal involvement that would strengthen your abilities and accept total responsibility for success or failure of the outcome. 

Expose yourself continually to scrutiny and then feel the pain of growth to your whole being. 

The words 'surrender', 'giving up', and 'procrastination' are no longer part of your vocabulary, and subterfuge and meaningless activities are no longer your calling.

It is difficult to cross this barrier of commitment for a lifetime, but once you are on the other side, committed to your mission, your life takes on a new meaning. The indecisions and retreating of the past are gone, and the future presents a more worthy prize because the value of the life has equaled the value of the commitment. 

During your life you will contend of course with many changes. I have a simple card in my wallet of my written vision together with the attributes of this program to keep me on course. 

Interestingly, my vision has never changed. My will has not diminished. The goal is ever before me and the mission is my everyday companion. With the help of God, my destiny of the third millennium will be achieved.

### Eight Principles of Creating Strong Willpower

To fulfill this commitment is going to take enormous willpower. When the heat is on and others turn their backs on you, there are principles that I have found over forty years that have always brought me through. Understand that God is committed to your development and the will underlies the integrity of the soul. 

Learn now the following 8 principles of creating a strong willpower:

**1. Understand the cost benefit ratio** 
- A strong will develops character whereas a weak will is the enemy to character. 
- A strong will creates self esteem but a weak will give shame.
- A strong will builds relationship whereas a weak will destroys trust.
- A strong will helps whereas a weak will hurts. 

**2. Remove the mystique.**
- A strong will is natural not supernatural and can be clearly understood if you make a commitment to yourself then you must develop the integrity to keep it.

**3. Clarify your values.**
- What are the most important things in life to you? Put a value measurement on each one.

**4. Open your mind to a new thought.** 
- Have an intellectual stretch occasionally and put your life up for scrutiny.
    
**5. Make the commencement easy.** 
- Take time out to be alone and dream and write a short biography of what you would like your life to be. Then write down the names of all your friends and alongside, write how you would like them to describe you.

**6. Begin the formula.**
- Develop clarity of thought and an uncluttered lifestyle and know what track you want to run on.

**7. Developing the formula.**
- For six weeks only, commit yourself only to that you will finish.

**8. Owning the formula**
- Know your life's values and document what you want to do with them by building an acceptable plan.

Within each individual there is a fascinating struggle between the will to do and the confusion in finding a clear straight line from where you are to your ultimate commitment. 

We understand, of course, that all people build their own worlds by the commitments they make and the balance of the victories of success and the whims of mediocrity and failure. If you abdicate the right to choose, then you lose a supreme power that choice provides, and you forfeit your freedom. A meaningful commitment has an inbuilt responsibility that makes it difficult for you to escape that choice.

What will take place during the study of this program will depend upon your real commitment to your own personal growth as you learn to avoid the fuzzy thinking that creates work and burns up energy with very few results. 

To make a life commitment requires many things but above all else it requires the ability to focus and imagine with serious and ambitious intent. This can only happen if the initiator has some effective means of approaching the thought process which often hides itself in simplicity. 

The mixture that produces the final result must clearly point the way ahead and at the same time provide additional stimuli along the way. Such are the building blocks of a life commitment.

These principles will create for you the real power to perform by replacing the illusory power that contains the imaginary flirtations of greatness and optimism based on fantasy with new insight, clear direction, and more control over your future. 

As an added bonus, you'll find that the principles already learned in this destiny of the third millennium will free up time zones because it improves straight line strategic thought towards growth and problem solving by using the principles of life commitment. Nothing clears vagueness and vacillation away so quickly as a committed life. It is avoided to protect the status quo and to circumvent the unpleasantness of truth. Ignorance will not cover its powerful force, nor will intellect explain away its vital message. All who endure for time will look into its awesome face and bow beneath its mighty presence. But to stop observe and use with care its great measurement is both wise and courageous.

### Endeavors Filters

Whatever your life commitment is, ask these principal questions in respect to all of your endeavors:

#### 1. What Is The Measured End Result Required?

Now we dealt with this in a somewhat different way in lesson 2. Here is a further development as a basic bottom line question that demands a descriptive objective. The clearer the description and the more accurate the measurement the easier it becomes to assess the equipment to get the job done. 

If our objective is to perform then we must avoid abstract terms as descriptive language for achievement. Words like big, small, or as soon as possible are relative terms for general conversation but are not destiny measurable terms focused on specific targets and should be avoided where definite results are required. Remember that the end may not justify the means but the means that are used do create the end result and the only way that means can be identified is to present it in clear descriptive language what you want to achieve. Why not take some time out and write a clear simple description of what is finally expected. This will provide a clear strong foundation to build on and will help you to create an uncluttered lifestyle for the future!

#### 2. Are There Any Checkpoints That Facilitate Early Correction Of Mistakes?

What is required at this point is to take an imaginary walk through the process of acquiring your objective, taking time to examine each bend in the road, reflecting on any past experience that could guide you in creating short term holding patterns that would allow for inspection and perspective. After having discovered the checkpoints create a set of guard ian rules to protect the ground already taken. Do not permit the excitement of the chase or emotion of the moment to distract you. Bear in mind that even sports, governments and the sciences have set of special rules to protect them and allow the sound workings of their endeavors. 

So why not create for your venture areas of ethics, culture, management and finance that will provide a set of principles to work within which will be valuable as you gain experience throughout your life. 

We all need to be willing to have the courage to recognize and admit a mistake. To cut losses may involve some initial personal pain but it may also in the long run prove to be far more productive. 

Moving ahead against an irritating permanent or reoccurring problem will slow down your efforts and even grow and become a major challenge that could have been avoided by earlier attention. 

If you develop the habit of correcting problems early and fixing or removing them permanently your progress will never be seriously impaired.

#### 3. Is The Path Clear and Direct?

To guard against misdirection we must be careful not to allow parochial interest or ego to divert us against our better judgment. We must realize that each misdirection weakens the total substance of our efforts and ultimately will cause us to miss the mark. 

We cannot assume that a misdirection will correct itself although chopping and changing and trying perilous shortcuts may seem exciting and attractive and appear to be less painful they are bound to be less defective in the end. 

The temptation to move away from the critical path is ever present. Seemingly greener fields will always look more attractive but once a direction has been assessed, planned and committed to, all else must be considered of no consequence until your final goal or objective is met. 

Keep to the clear concise directions that provide a straight intelligent path and avoid the circumnavigation of endless pursuits.

#### 4. Is There A Real Or Accepted Sacrifice To Make?

Whenever sacrifice is mentioned in a business or any other situation it usually conjures up images of neglecting your family or mortgaging your house or working eighteen hours a day towards a high risk venture and hopefully towards an equally high return. 

Only you can assess a sacrifice that fits into your own personal value system. The risk of neglecting family is usually not on the list, but the risk should be considered as a possibility if things go wrong and commitments creep up on you. 

Make some firm decisions about what sacrifices you are prepared to make without minimizing the impact it might have on your lifestyle and financial security. As a contrast to what I have just said, I would like to suggest to you here that if there is no sacrifice to make then, your vision may be too small. The risk of a detriment or loss is often as big a motivator as the benefits of victory, And so it soon becomes evident that pain or sacrifice emphasizes the the lesson and makes them more memorable. 

Take some comfort that sacrifice very often indicates a value you have placed on the project. And so in a sense a sacrifice truly evaluated indicates in a way the value of your own personal life commitment and can turn a project into a major evaluation exercise.

#### 5. Does The Value Of The Objective Reflect The Pain Of Commitment?

This whole system of 'life commitment thinking' is based on value to you as an individual and what in the way of personal attributes can be traded for a more profitable attainment. Just as beauty is in the eye of the beholder, so value to one individual will be different and distinct from value to another.

In business of course this differs somewhat as value is usually assessed in terms of profits, quality and timing.

There are two types of pain: voluntary and involuntary pain. Involuntary pain is one that is thrust upon you by circumstances where voluntary pain is what you have decided to inflict upon yourself or business in an effort to create a benefit. 

To appreciate more fully the relationship between the pain and success an evaluation must be made in terms of something more permanent than immediate needs. 

For instance, the pain of study in engineering or medicine over a few years is infinitesimal relative to a lifetime building and healing and teaching others to do the same. The multiplication factor can literally go on for centuries. What assessment do we make of the person who is locked into poverty but who works, studies, sweats to finally get his family out of that situation and in doing so creates a new generation with a new expansive mindset that could last for many generations to come. 

For a value system to be accurate, we need a philosophy of living. The only measure of real value is permanence.

#### 6. Will It Provide A Definite Solution To A Definite Need?

A life or business that becomes overwhelmed with consuming activities that lead nowhere or cannot be recognized by any substance must be avoided at all cost.

Some people and businesses are so desperate to have a cause or challenge to grasp that that they will use questionable opportunities and will even overstretch in an effort to obtain some status action or expansion. 
In 'life commitment thinking' everything must be measured against time, return and longevity so that meaningless and wasteful endeavors will not steal precious time, abilities and resources that could be assigned to some more profitable or more enjoyable pursuit. 

Be careful not to fantasize a solution to a disappearing or obsolete need. In other words, don't fix something that's not broken and don't expand an irritation or create a problem so that you can fix it. There are much better ways to obtain fulfillment. 

It appears that every now and then someone ignores hard evidence and wants to prove everything and everybody else wrong. Stop. Do not confuse your desire to do with the reality of the need. Always keep in mind that if you want to create a solution, then make it permanent. It may take longer and even cost more, but your value system will give the balance.

#### 7. Can You Give It Your Best Without Reservation?

We are all given life to live, with a God given capacity to dream what would seem to be impossible dreams. We observe over the passage of time all global records are broken with more of the same to come. Humankind is yet to exhibit its best. The key to the magnificent world changing dreams lies passive within each one of us. 

Giving of your best includes ingredients of time, talent, and effort, which must beg the question, are you prepared to trade your life for your vision? Stop now and reflect and let your rule self step forward. Any hesitation or vacillation at this point could indicate fear. Fear of what? It would be very wise to find out as this will prevent you from trading your life for undervalued goods. Just as every good business enterprise counts a rule measured cost of any venture, which at times must include the human factor, so we must evaluate our commitment level in respect to ourselves towards our destiny of the 3rd millennium.

#### 8. Do You Have A Better Plan And If You Do, Are You Prepared To Risk All In It?

It's often very easy to be critical about a plan created and produced by someone else, but it's quite different situation when the total responsibility for its success or failure rests on your shoulders. 

Often when I have heard rumblings from discontented participants about a project, I have asked about their plans and the commitment, level of finance and responsibility that they would be prepared to risk, only to be met by a confusion of meaningless platitudes. 

But there are those rare occasions when you do have a plan, and you do believe in it, and are assured of its success. Then you have reached the commitment level because a good plan deserves your all and a bad plan deserves nothing. 

In essence we are talking about trading a particular non redeemable segment of your life so double check your facts. 

Is it the best plan you can put together or can you improve it? 

Always try to minimize the risk factor by investigative plans but never overlook the fact that the more you insulate yourself from risk the more you insulate yourself against success. Without a thoroughly thought out plan you have reduced yourself to the animal level of instinct and deprived yourself of the benefits of the creative genius that you possess.

#### 9. Can You Look Failure In The Face And Start Again With Vigor?

It has often been said that you can judge people's character by the way they respond to failure. 

To some, it seems to crush to the point that they will never rise again and they tend to dwell from that point in a cocoon of protection never venturing out again in the activity of pursuit. 

To others it acts like an adrenaline shot pressing them further on pursuing and adventuring seeking to find a new challenge with which they can wrestle. 

Personally I have found that at the point of every failure, an opportunity for change or progress was always present if sought with positive expectancy. 

A failure or a disappointment can act as a bottom line barometer by giving you strong signals that you're on the wrong track. Because you are interested in a life commitment, you understand that being at the bottom, there is only one direction available to you and that is to go up. 

So be objective and remember failures must act as springboards to success. The only time you're really finished is when you refuse to respond. You only lose when you give up. Of course failures are just one of life's lessons which have to be learned and when the lesson is learned it provides a set of firm rules towards success. Then you move upwards towards an even more challenging lesson that will expand your thought process just as it invigorates you.

#### 10. Are You Prepared To Out-think Your Present Capacity?

There is and there always will be a better way to do things. Continued concentrated thought will always provide opportunities that were overlooked by adolescent thinkers who were not prepared to wrestle with the big picture and the capacities of their own minds. 

The way to stretch your thinking is to relate to the unrelatable by crisscrossing ideas and by bouncing your thoughts against others who disagree with you and who provoke more insights than those who placidly tend to agree. 

Expand your thoughts by thinking years in advance and imagine how you may even need to adapt or even move into a different field of endeavor. 

Thinking with positive expectancy is probably the hardest of hard work to do and is usually avoided and is boundless possibilities left unexplored. I've always found that several hours of focused thought towards a project or a problem with space to allow for reflection followed by more concentrated thought with discipline always produces the exciting and the unexpected. 

Sometimes it is even best to sit back and allow your mind to glide as if in flight and in time it may find a resting place in the form of a new idea or concept that could not have been possible by other means. 

Any time spent in deep thought towards a predetermined measurable objective will always produce a valuable response. 

So think in dimensions you have never tried before and through the multiples of such activities, you will easily outthink your present capacity.

#### 11. Will You Adopt A Life Commitment To Always Seek And Find With Accuracy Your Personal Destiny? 

The Bible truth 'seek and you shall find' seeks to invigorate the human species towards a search for immortality. It also contains what I believe to be a life commitment truth that can be applied to everyday living. 

The seeker is always searching for that which has potential and has in mind that which ordinary observers tend to overlook. 

'Life commitment thinking' goes right to the core of every problem and into the treasury of every opportunity, not seeking argument but rather seeking truth. With truth comes the essence of all that we find that has endurance. 

To be continually successful, 'life commitment thinking' must become an indispensable habit in everyday living which requires that you be real, be true, be alive, be great, And see life in a clearer perspective by aiming at the focal point in every target that clarifies, measures and simplifies. 

Never accept the smoke screen of pseudo rationale or the explanations of well intended or verbal camouflage but rather ask the questions. Keep asking the questions, dig for reasons, Seek fresh alternatives and look for unspoken agendas until you find the truth. 

And the truth will indeed set you free to become the best in whatever you seek to do.

#### 12. Will Your Life Create A Permanent Change Or A Benefit To Someone Else?

In the final principle in' life commitment thinking' we should reflect on two important aspects of our time together before we conclude this lesson. 

The first aspect is a secret personal aspiration that we all have that rarely come to the surface for us all to see. This somewhat hidden agenda has a deeper meaning. It is not to be aired for public scrutiny less by the sharing, as delicate threads be broken and our inner fragile ego be forced to choose another course. 

The second aspect is our public personal or business aspirations which are up for scrutiny and which tend to keep us publicly, at least, circumspect in our perceptions and dealings.

In both areas, wouldn't it be a great pity if after all your planning, all your efforts, when you pass on, nothing of permanence is left behind.

In your long term planning, seek to incorporate a permanence factor by asking yourself the question, how many years will this last without my direct involvement? And who and how many will benefit in the long term? Be honest with yourself. 


### Conclusion

Finally, what can you do to create a better world? Whatever all the schemes and dreams that we fulfill, to be truly great, we must be benefactors, which in essence means we must leave behind something that will be a benefit to others.

The benefit of life commitment thinking will also allow you to stretch and grow to unlimited heights and will provide some insulation against worry, uncertainty, change, pressure, misunderstanding, and distrust. It will act as a barrier against poverty and provide some control reserve to protect you from recklessness and give you the courage to think big. My wish for you is that you will think beyond and above the normalities of life and respond to others and their needs with compassion and generosity and with the simple understanding that when god gave you the power to freely commit your life, he also gave you the power to love.
