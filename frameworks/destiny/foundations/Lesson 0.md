### The World Changes Creed
  
Destiny of the Third Millennium, commencing with the World Changes Creed.
  
  - **The Dream:** The dream, a sense of grandeur towards life.
  
  - **The Vision:** The vision, the depth, capacity, and measurable impact of a dream.
  
  - **The Commitment:** The commitment, the acceptance acceptance of pain as a cost towards benefit.
  
  - **The Goal:** The goal, the dedication to think, plan, meditate, and learn.
  
  - **The Mission:** The mission, the purity, purity, forbearance, and fearlessness of character.
  
  - **The Destiny:** The destiny, the imperative to exchange a life for a moral cause that will endure.
