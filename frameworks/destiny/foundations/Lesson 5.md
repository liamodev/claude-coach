# Lesson Five: The Mission
  
The purity, forbearance, and fearlessness of character.

**Biblical Base for the Mission:**
Habakkuk 2:2, 3 'Then the Lord answered me and said: 'Write the vision and make it plain on tablets, that he may run who reads it. For the vision is yet for an appointed time: but at the end it will speak and it will not lie. Though it tarries. wait for it: because it will surely come. it will not tarry.'

The mission is 'the purity, forbearance, and fearlessness of character', which determines the ethics and the mechanics of the journey towards your goal.

This fifth lesson can be a blueprint for success and priority living that will clarify your direction and make the journey of life more enjoyable. The following information will guide you into a take charge lifestyle that puts you at the control levers of your life. For some this may become unsettling because there is no let out clause to release you from personal and corporate accountability. 

A mission statement will stimulate you to think and assess yourself against opportunities that are available. Lesson five. It will provide security and a standard against which a changing world and personal circumstances can be measured. It will provide clear guidelines that have been carefully thought out in advance, where there was more time for reflection and assessment. A mission statement also allows you in difficult times and crises to fall back on the realities of who you are and what you stand for in respect to your responsibilities and obligations, as well as assisting your own preservation. 

Very often in a mission situation the final goal or objectives become secondary and the journey preeminent. That is why you will often hear very successful people say the journey was more exhilarating than the destination. Sticking to a plan requires a sense of mission, and without it, many good intentions are never brought to fruition. We often spend all our time, thought, and efforts focusing on the final destination without taking into consideration the quality of the journey.
  
- A mission statement can be described as a written statement of purpose and behavioral boundaries worthy of a life pursuit.
- A mission statement sets a pattern of behavior that exhibits the character of an organizational person.
- A mission statement is a style of pursuit.
- A mission statement provides the ethos for an organization or a person.
- A mission statement reflects the spirit rather than the law of a person or organization.
- A mission statement creates the relationship culture of a person or an organization.
- A mission statement is a quasi trustee of a person or an organization.
- A mission statement is a secure anchor in the stormy seas of life.

If you need to try and adjust your mission statement to suit a particular set of circumstances, this can only mean that:
1. The mission statement was not thoroughly thought out in the first place
2. Your value system has changed
3. The destination is still not clear

A mission statement that is ambiguous, sloppy, and open to manipulation always provides a way for the unscrupulous to enter and the uncommitted to exit. 

Beware of long explanations and justifications with respect to changing and manipulating or overriding a mission statement.

A great pianist was once approached by the mirer who said, I would do anything to be able to play a piano like you. In response, the pianist said, no, you wouldn't. I still get approached by people throughout the world seeking the secrets of success. In a nutshell, I would like to share one of the secrets with you. It is this: **Success is a willingness to bear pain, not as a masochist or just to prove you can endure, but rather by necessity in the process of becoming what you would like to be.** 

Pain comes from failure, disappointment, embarrassment, and the continuing thrust to achieve your goals, often facing setbacks and major changes in events, which can put you behind even where you started. The pain of achieving success continually day after day, month after month, and year after year as you try to remove the camouflage which conceals your key to success. Pain is not being able to get out of yourself that which you know you can produce.

Generally, mankind is seduced to the comfort zone, but victories are rarely found in the comfort zone. As a matter of fact, it appears that most achievers in any field are found in the pain arena. 

Beware of the camouflage of mediocrity, which knows the edge of success but never ventures past the still waters of safety and comfort. 

Do you buckle under stress? Do you avoid confrontation? Do you avoid discomfort to the detriment of your performance?

Seeking to avoid confrontation or discomfort is natural and will provide protection from harm. But the possibility of success in any field presupposes that conflict, opposition, competition, and difficulties will always arise. If you find that this is not for you, it may indicate that followership rather than leadership is the best course for you to take. 

For those of you who have aspiration towards leadership in any field, I would suggest that you be mindful of the fact that leadership presupposes there will be 'followership', and often we need to understand the ethos of 'followership' before we can become a good leader.

### Putting Your Personal Mission Statement Together
  
Now I come to the detail of creating your own dynamic mission statement that works. I will put in the mechanical form all the facets of the mission statement so that it becomes a specific practical and durable document. 

The following eight steps I will try and improve a method that has been tested over many years. To help you in creating a mission statement, I will show you here how it fits into a dynamic plan. The eight steps are:

1. The goal
2. The vehicle
3. The creed
4. The commitment
5. The qualification
6. The positioning
7. The assessment
8. The destination


#### 1. The Goal
  
The goal that you documented in lesson 4 needs to be fully stated at this point so that the journey by way of your mission statement, can be formalized to create a working, living, moving vehicle.
  
#### 2. The Vehicle
  
To make any journey possible, there must be a vehicle that can carry the load to the goal. The mission's role, of course, is to mobilize a vehicle towards a goal. Hence, the choice of a vehicle must be suited to the goal. For instance, it would not be compatible to have a vehicle that restricted travel if your goal demanded international markets, or conversely to demand a vehicle for international travel when your goal was primarily local. So in short, the vehicle must be subject to the goal. The mission has a task of energizing the vehicle to reach the goal. It must be obvious by now that the vehicle is another part of the structure that requires careful selection, because it will directly influence both the speed and the style of the mission. A simple question should be asked when considering your vehicle. For instance, if the vehicle that you are using as your business has to do with oil, then you need to make the long term decision whether you are in the oil or the energy business, because eventually electricity and some hitherto unknown energy sources will be discovered. The decision you initially make could lead either to obsolescence or to future opportunities. The vehicle.
  
**The Vehicle - Suggested mission statement:** *The vehicle to reach my goal will always be the focus of my financial multiplication and protection by which all decisions must be tempered.*
  
#### 3. The Creed
  
Here you will need to use all the information that you have gathered about yourself during the study of this program. You will notice that each area is sectionalized to give you a clearer formula covering all aspects necessary to equip yourself to form and write a plan for this journey. The creed need to express some personal desires that hopefully will remain constant throughout your life. The character, beliefs, and intangibles will provide for you at all times throughout your mission the sustaining thrust to continue against what would seem insurmountable odds while still retaining the attributes of morality, which will promote and provide an anchor in the stormy seas of life.
  
**The Creed - Suggested mission statement:** *I am a person of destiny, and I know what I am prepared to exchange my life for, and I herein declare my mission to reach my ultimate goal. I will always maintain a positive temperament and present myself in speech, dress, behavior, and lifestyle as a distinctive major player in the arena of life. Loyalty and commitment to my family and friends will be my continuing assets. My quest for knowledge and information to meet my needs and overcome my weaknesses will be met by discipline in equal measure.*
  
#### 4. The Commitment
  
This is in the area of discipline and growth, and is usually the area of breakdown. To sum it up quite simply, it is a matter of integrity. There is no honor in making commitments and then later breaking them. This part of your mission statement is the essence of what really happens, and without the determination to fulfill this part of your mission statement, all else is futile. In very clear terms, means fulfilling the substance of every part of your mission statement and turning good thoughts and emotion into power. As an interesting side issue here, the biblical word for God's love to man is agape, which is a discipline not dependent on emotion.
  
I could name scores of friends and acquaintances who started out on the road to success bright eyed and full of great dreams, enthusiasm, and optimism, only to fall by the wayside because they did not commit themselves to the price that needed to be paid to finish the course. The pain you will endure in your commitment is unavoidable. Bear it, and weakness will exit from your character and mission.
  
**The Commitment - Suggested mission statement:** *My commitment to complete my mission is as sacred to me as life itself and is accepted as a great gift of power. So I will make commitments carefully with understanding that all I am and all I hope to be will be embodied in this great treasure.*
  
#### 5. The Qualification
  
The qualification that would be necessary to reach the suggested goal would be many, but could loosely be described in the following way: To build a large business enterprise, organisation, personal achievement, influence or power, start with learning to build successfully on a small scale. This will, in a small way, do what you ultimately want to do on a larger scale at a later date. A working knowledge of economics, accountancy, politics or science will give the theory, but the successful outworking of the theory in practical terms will provide the inescapable proof that you are in fact ready to go on to a higher grade. 

Assess the qualifications you need to reach your goal to enable you to create the means to reach it, always acknowledging that there will be unknowns and new developments. At all cost, avoid jumping into areas which you do not have proven experience. Carefully study each step to ensure that no ground will be lost by stepping out into the unknown without reserves.
  
**The Qualification - Suggested mission statement:** *I will enrich my life by learning the lessons of economics, management, entrepreneurship, leadership, or whatever is required by exposing myself to continually higher levels of practical and theoretical understanding. When I cannot prove my lessons and experience, I will repeat them until I can. Until by automatic response, I use the principles that I have found to work. My financial reserves and knowledge will prevent me from losing too much ground should I make a mistake or should misfortune come upon me. The eight points of my dynamic mission statement that worked will always be subject to qualification and understanding, and any fracture or infringement will promptly be corrected. All my qualifications will be assessed and scrutinized in the clear light of two inseparable qualities, truth and proof. I will seek always to minimize my mistakes by maximizing my clarity.*
  
#### 6. The Positioning
  
Now let's move to number six, the positioning. This part of your dynamic mission statement that works deals with the positioning of yourself in the parade of life to assist you to play a major role in the acquisition of your destiny goal. It must be obvious that if you remove yourself from where the action is, then you are going to find it impossible to be successful. Do not overlook creating friendships and allies that you may find useful and interesting as you grow, and try to deal with people you respect or even like, making the length of the journey more pleasant. 
  
Seek always to place yourself in a crucial role wherever information relative to your challenge becomes easier to obtain. As an information receiver, you automatically become an informed center of influence. As part of your environmental structure, pay attention to your permanent location and travel schedule, seeking to develop second and third person influence, which will enhance your real and perceived interests. Be just as careful in avoiding any involvements that would conflict with your creed. By doing so, you will send a clear picture of who you are, what you stand for, and where you intend to go.
  
**The Qualification - Suggested mission statement:** *I will seek to position myself at the pivotal point of progress and change, always maintaining the strength which stability gives, and sensitive to know how it can be a benefit towards the achievement of my mission. My friendships and liaisons will reflect and enhance my direction, location, mobility, always seeking to place me in timing and opportunity in the most crucial position. A careful selection of people, places, and events will represent at all times my desire to influence and to be influenced toward my objectives.*
  
#### 7. The Assessment
  
Real measurements will need to be undertaken to assess the progress of your mission at regular intervals. The goal that you documented in lesson four has specifics and measurements, which must be broken down in relation to quantitative time frames to evaluate progress. Sometimes the assessment may be more tangible, such as positions. At other times, the progress may be more a matter of influence or positioning, ready to produce more tangible results later. Each of your mission moves must reflect the progress towards your destination. Otherwise, the estimated time of arrival may never come. To suggest a mission statement without any form of regular assessment is to invite failure and may give the impression of activity without objectivity.
  
**The Assessment - Suggested mission statement:** *My mission has monthly time, substance, and information checks in direct relationship to progress, all calculated in numerical terms to avoid inaccuracies. I will always pay particular attention to personal growth assessments and spheres of influence that are needed to reach each point of my assessment. Major checks in all areas will be conducted on an annual basis and longer term objectives will be carefully created so that they are reflected in the overall mission.*

#### 8. The Destination
  
No journey can be completed without knowing and focusing on the destination. The whole purpose of creating your mission statement is to arrive at a predetermined destination called a goal in a measurable and specific way within a time frame that was predetermined. A clear look at the destination and its exact time, shape, and form need to be clearly visible in your mind to keep you on course and to prevent deviations. One of the unusual aspects of life is that we sometimes get more than we aim for. Because when we commence the mission, we are unaware of the personal changes that will take place in our capabilities and capacities. Hence, sometimes the destination or the goal can finish up being much larger than that which was predetermined. The destination.

**The Assessment - Suggested mission statement:** *Realizing that my focus must be twofold, that is, on the clarity of the final goal and the mission to achieve it, I must also sense and enhance the growth of those personal qualities necessary for both the mission and the goal.*

### Developing a Corporate or Organization Mission Statement
  
It's somewhat the same that a corporation faces a different kind of scrutiny with dependence placed more on the team than the individual. 

In this part of the lesson, we will move from the singular into the multiple in terms of people, opportunities, complications, and variables. 

What we as individuals may wish to do must now be tempered by what we can accomplish through others, including bureaucracies and the barriers it builds, as well as a force it can be for progress if, at the commencement, plans for stimulation and growth are built in. 

While many of the steps towards a personal and corporate mission statement are similar, they will differ in content because one deals primarily with an individual, while the other deals with separate and distinctive entity known as a corporation or an organization. As noted above, a corporation faces a different kind of scrutiny with dependence placed more on the team than the individual. In placing the emphasis on the team, I'm not suggesting that all participants are equally responsible, but rather that the leader of the team must provide a climate in which all participants can become part of the whole. When talent and desire coincide in an individual, then that person can be fulfilled by being fully stretched.
  
#### Getting The Bits Together
The following ten points will provide a simple benchmark that will allow you in an organized way to pull the elementary threads together in preparation for creating your corporate mission statement:

1. Clarity of direction
2. Care and understanding of human nature
3. Comprehensive study of markets and trends
4. Tenacity for both quality and growth
5. Knowledge of short- and long-term economics
6. Vision for new concepts and developments
7. Commitment to the long-term challenge
8. Ability to cope with continued criticism and loneliness
9. Preparedness to give others the credit
10. Selection of directors


#### CLARITY OF DIRECTION
The mission you are about to commence is like any journey and it must have absolute clarity of direction. As you prepare to write out a corporate mission statement. the objective is that the people at the lowest level of involvement must understand very clearly what is meant and how they personally fit into the overall plan. Any ambiguity of any kind will provide a toe-hold for misunderstanding and mistakes which can bum up time, money and trust and that will inevitably sabotage progress.

Clarity of direction provides a security blanket for those who do not have the positioning or the privilege to be involved in the major decision-making that controls their very livelihoods. For a medium or large organization the assurance of clarity will pay large economic dividends because the workforce knows the road ahead. With mixed signals there is a great deal of wastage of energy.

#### CARE AND UNDERSTANDING OF HUMAN NATURE
All of us have heard stories of people who have worked tirelessly for a long time with a company or an organization and were convinced that they were the linchpins holding the whole place together. only to receive little or no appreciation for their loyalty. talent and effort. Some left. only to find that the gap was quickly filled, while others sought and found a bigger and better opportunity, leaving the previous employer floundering.

People in general terms can be divided into four distinct groups:
1. **Maintenance:** those who want security without responsibility.
2. **Stabilizers:** those who desire some form of medium levels of responsibility and are prepared to work for it.
3. **De-stabilizers:** those who move from position to position seeking to enhance their talents and provide them to the highest bidder. They can be very helpful for a short time. but become restless and must move on. They can be found at every level.
4. **Growth:** those who distinguish themselves by their commitment. intelligence. and hunger to accept responsibility and new challenges.

Careful understanding and appreciation for the special needs of each of these groups w ill unify them in the mission, and, in doing so, provide a power force of awesome dimensions.

#### COMPREHENSIVE STUDY OF MARKETS AND TRENDS
In the creating of your corporate or organizational mission statement. you will require at least a working knowledge of markets and trends. I would like to add another two important elements, particularly if you are going to market internationally. and these are culture and law.

To know your market and how you can possess it proves the often quoted, and I believe true. statement that nothing continually happens in business until something is sold". Whatever your goal is to be. its final success will be to a large degree dependent on a comprehensive understanding of markets and trends. So often a market that appears to be strong and in full flight can collapse because insufficient attention was paid to trends in style, obsolescence or marketing strategies. Attention must also be paid to immediate start-up markets and trends, with a further serious focus on future innovative trends.

When moving into fresh markets, even in your own country, beware of possible cultural and legal differences that may impede or even prevent market shares.

This information can easily be obtained from the appropriate government departments of the country where your interests are, or from tourist, migration, government investment or political literature.


#### TENACITY FOR BOTH QUALITY AND GROWTH
One would expect the simple desire for quality would naturally guarantee growth. But economic history has shown quite clearly that business has very few guarantees. and the best product may have other short comings. For instance, if the quality of your product causes it to be too expensive. or the delivery is not consistent, or the advertising and the outlets are inadequate, or it is too far ahead of its time, then the capacity for growth is very much diminished. There has to be a balanced pattern on price, quality. availability and awareness to ensure that quality and quantity are best exploited. To start and stop a market mid-stream because attention has not been paid to all of these elements will cause considerable expense and difficulties. It will also have a negative effect on those in your organization: because interruptions signal insecurity and uncertain progress.

The aggressiveness of the corporation will keep it on the cutting edge of growth. and the added ingredient of quality will ensure it remains there. It comes from the top down. not from the bottom up. To achieve success. the mission statement and the provocateur (ie the missioner) must possess a permanent. demonstrable and enduring appetite for quality and growth.

#### A KNOWLEDGE OF SHORT-AND LONG-TERM ECONOMICS
It has often been said, 'If you put a salesman in the same room as an accountant. you can be assured of conflict.' The other cliche about those who handle the financial accounts is. 'They do not kick the goals, they only keep the score.' In spite of what is said, I believe we need to be reminded in business that a good accountant with a short- and long-term in-depth knowledge of economics can often clear the fiscal pathway ahead,,. alerting you to danger signals and preventing ground already claimed being lost.

The best kind of economic advice for you to obtain is from those who are already demonstrably rich in finance and achievement and have maintained that position over a long term.

What seems to be a good economic strategy today can turn into an economic nightmare tomorrow. Here, then, are some simple but effective economic principles that I have found both protective and expansive for any business:

1. Maintain control and do not relinquish it at any price.
2. Beware of economic involvement where debt can expand by fluctuating currencies, interest rates or penalties.
3. Always protect the gains that you have made.
4. Never risk your future on the prospects of loan.
5. Always have protective capital reserves.

#### VISIONS FOR NEWCONCEPTS AND DEVELOPMENT
There are some rare businesses that have survived by doing the same thing over several generations. But during the passage of time their market diminishes, and their products or services are no longer required. You will find that changes in lifestyles, materials and new products finally make them obsolete. Any mission that does not seek, by investigative development, new ways, new uses and/or alternative products is doomed to failure and cannot stand the test of time.

Create an atmosphere of innovation by encouraging adventure within the organization, even to the point of holding special meetings at every level of employment for that purpose. Many times I have seen management wrestle with problems that they are too close to, only to find that someone in the lower echelons of the corporation could easily provide the answer.

Develop an attitude of openness to ideas and a reward system for those which are useful. Bear in mind that the methods of today are not necessarily the methods of tomorrow. although there are rare occasions where you can go back to the past and resurrect an old method. and by adaptation and innovation make it useful again. The key here is to look into the future and try to visualize what will be needed and how that need can be met.

#### COMMITMENTTOTHE LONG-TERM CHALLENGE
A personal mission statement allows you time to relax and even drift for a while: but the corporate mission does not permit that luxury. A corporate mission statement automatically has certain tightly held restrictions that need to be assessed against a long-term commitment that will affect the life of the leader in particular, and in a slightly lesser way, those in the upper echelons of management.

Because of the responsibility to creditors. shareholders or supporters and the market place, any large businesses or organizations cannot go into neutral for respite like an individual can. The commitment that the corporation or organization has to those it employs and those that support it. which. I believe. is a sacred trust, means that vigilance must be the watch-word at all times. Just as some new employees commence their work today: so retirees are relinquishing their work at the same time. and so the process continues with all trusting the expressed mission statement in order to keep their commitment to their own families and their mortgages. What a committed mission can do is to put a kinder face on capitalism, and in the process everyone benefits.

#### THE ABILITY TO COPE WITH CONTINUED CRITICISM AND LONELINESS
Because this subject needs to be mentioned again. the leader (the missioner), as an individual, will always be the object of criticism and may often feel somewhat lonely because of the authoritative position he/she holds, which automatically creates isolation. A corporate or organizational mission statement comes into its own in these two areas. It provides a known direction which minimizes criticism and has the allegiance of an agreed group of people. This provides something of the comfort of a crowd all aiming at the same target. Often the management and the whole group will close ranks to protect others from criticism because they see not only the effect on the person criticized but also the reflection on the total corporation.

A leader who sticks to the principles of the mission statement. in the face of outside opposition. may even find himself or herself suddenly given hero status because the group feels that. in his or her standing up for the mission, they themselves have been protected. Never underestimate the group power and support that comes from adhering to a mission statement. even if it has. by its implications. created some negative results. The fact is that if it is maintained it will produce security and confidence.

#### PREPAREDNESS TO GIVE OTHERS THE CREDIT
A mission statement that does not recognize the efforts of all who participate will not stimulate the efforts of all. The operation of a corporation must always be recognized as a team effort. while not minimizing in any way the role of the leader or leaders.

The essence of good leadership is to provide clear direction. continued motivation and long-term plans and then to become the hero by giving a great deal of the credit to others in the team. In no way does this diminish the public stature of the leader or leaders but it can enhance it. and if they have a limited public personality, it provides the mystique that ultimately sets them apart. The best response to any successful corporate campaign by the leader is, without exception. to point to all the team players, even naming individuals if special distinctions need to be made. Loyalty always follows genuine appreciation.

#### SELECT BOARD MEMBERS WISELY
I am of the belief that there should be two board levels to address the stability, policy making, fiscal responsibility and planning of every corporation. The first group should have the legal power and the responsibility for the total operation. In my opinion. these should be people with a diversity of interests and professions who already have their own successful corporations with a proven long-term track record. Rarely do I consider business people who have retired because they have a dinosaur's reaction to time, and their decisions usually reflect that. I almost never have on the legal board of directors those who are employed by others, irrespective of how high up a ladder they are, because (a) their time is not their own, and (b) they are not used to making plans and decisions involving their own money.

The second group of directors should be an advisory board with no legal or company responsibilities. This may include some well-known names. They must have the ability to contribute meaningfully to discussions and plans. and should also constitute those who are being considered for future full board membership.

An advisory board can be a powerful tool that expands the knowledge and public perception of a
corporation without the cumbersomeness of creating a legal bureaucracy. The advisory board members can hold their positions for a limited time. allowing for the release of some advisory board members, or for retaining others. When choosing a board of directors always obtain a commitment and agreement to the mission statement before appointment.

Always have a simple means of retiring board members if they do not fit in. Remember, a disgruntled board member who stays on. because a plan has not been put into place for a swift. silent removal. can do untold damage to an organization. It is easy to put people on a board, but often difficult to remove them!

But as a selection from a number of corporate goals is probably not transferable to another corporation or organization. Again, I encourage you to follow the process, not the project. A corporate mission statement differs from a personal mission statement in three ways.
  
  1. It must be presented as a public and general mission statement for all who care to read it, which would include suppliers, customers, and all those working for or supporting the corporation or organization.
  
  2. There must be a fuller, more comprehensive working goals program for those charged with the responsibility of ensuring its full implementation.
  
  3. There need to be a sloganizing of the mission statement, either in part or in full, for inspiration, advertising, and public relations. The following eight steps to achievement, which are under the same heading as those in the personal mission statement, are distinctively different in their outworking in the area of corporate dynamics and achievement. 
  
 The variation from a personal mission statement is that the eight steps are not in the same order, and so number one becomes number two, which means a vehicle predates the goal. Some of the other steps have been changed, sometimes crisscrossing those which are in the personal mission statement formula. Keep in mind at all times that although the mission statement contains a goal, it is not a goals program and should only be used as a journey to fulfill the detailed goals program. The other noticeable difference in the corporate mission statement is its length, which is somewhat shorter than the personal mission statement because the corporate mission statement always had the added advantage of a major goals plan to support it. Also, areas of attention are not as easily to declare in short form.


Now we will list very simply the eight steps to cover in the preparation of a corporate mission statement. These are: one, the vehicle two, the goal three, the commitment four, the qualification five, the positioning six, the destination seven, the assessment and eight, the creed.

#### 1. The Corporate Vehicle
  
In a corporate structure, the vehicle or the product becomes a prominent item because all management, finance, marketing, and production has its focus on this as the primary reason for being. Without the vehicle, product or service, no corporate entity exists. With the selection of your vehicle, a great deal of consideration must be given to the limitations that are both inwardly and outwardly put upon it. For instance, if you're going to build a marketing corporation, some consideration needs to be given to what limit you will put on it. 

You may want to market kitchenware, but the decision needs to be made as to whether you are in the home kitchen supply business or the engineering and design business, understanding that the latter would provide more options for growth as opportunities present themselves. 

If, for instance you are manufacturing porcelain lavatory pans, are you going to restrict yourself to that or are you going to consider that you're in the bathroom accessory or the porcelain or glass molding business?

As an example, remember now, follow the process not the project, I would suggest that the finding and marketing of oil fuels could be your vehicle. Now the question that must be answered here is, am I in the fuel business or the energy business?. I'm sure we all see new types of fuel and energy sources ahead. Having decided that I'm in the energy business and this is my vehicle for my corporate mission statement, a sample corporate mission statement needs to be presented to cover all of these variables. 
  
**Suggested statement:** *The corporation has been established and will continue for the production and the marketing of energy.*
  
#### 2. The Corporate Goal
  
Once a vehicle has been established, then a goal with a time frame, size, and variables must be established. The corporate goal program must be set and agreed to by the leaders and confirmed by the Board of Directors. To be useful, it must be documented and detailed in short range, medium range, and long range planning. This part of the corporate mission statement should not be a details gold program, but it should present the direction. Let's look at a corporate goal with a suggested mission statement.
  
**Suggested statement:** *Our goal is to lead in the production of quality and diversified energy that will meet the market needs of a global economy.*
  
#### 3. The Corporate Commitment
  
The commitment to reach the corporate goal must be expressed in words that reflect discipline, in equal measure, and it must be spelled out in terms that can be easily grasped by all. A very necessary part of the corporate mission statement is to have it so directionally worded that it can be used as a motivational tool throughout the entire corporation. So thirdly, the corporate commitment. And here is the suggested mission statement.
  
**Suggested statement:** *The commitment to satisfy our corporate goal will be reflected in our effort and purpose and sustained by internal and external examination.*
  
#### 4. The Corporate Qualification
  
Reaching a corporate goal requires a mission that will have proven capable people and attainments to match the needs at every level. This can be expressed as a focal point in the corporate mission statement. In effect, it means that those who are chosen as key players must have the proven quality to lead, and in turn select quality methods and quality tools. It means that not only must the management of the field personnel be of quality, but that quality needs to be demonstrated at every level. The qualifications of personnel must continually be upgraded to position the corporation on the front line of new discoveries and techniques. The corporate qualification and a suggested mission statement.
  
**Suggested statement:** *Our personnel, product and service will accept as evidence of qualification the results in the marketplace.*
  
#### 5. The Corporate Positioning
  
The most important step in positioning is to be at the crucial point of timing and location of opportunities. The second step is to be as sure as you can that the perception of the advantage is equal to the fact. It is very important to have a plan to be involved in whatever is available that will put you in the box seat to observe opportunities so that you can capitalize on them. 

Get involved in the industry, science and government discussions that can help you keep in touch, while at the same time conduct in-depth investigations that could prove profitable to your own corporation. To reduce all of this to a corporate mission statement is difficult, but not impossible, so let's try. The corporate positioning. Now a suggested mission statement.
  
**Suggested statement:** *Our insightful projections and effective communication will provide many leadership advantages.*
  
#### 6. The Corporate Destination
  
The object of the corporate mission statement is to point clearly to the goal, which is the final destination. To reach a destination, a sense of practical purpose and growth must be built into the corporate mission statement, allowing all those involved to ask themselves the question, can I grow as the corporation grows? The destiny will at times change some of its features because of the unpredictable developments that are bound to occur, but we must be prepared for this. Build into the mission statement room for growth and variables for people, products, and services so that you can adapt to unexpected interruptions along the way. Now for the corporate destination with a suggested mission statement.
  
**Suggested statement:** *Understanding that progress ultimately brings change, we will refocus our corporate goals, thereby stimulating all who participate towards that target.*
  
#### 7. Corporation Assessment
  
The assessment of any corporation goal is accomplishment and profit. They will always come in that order. Without wise planning and economic prudence, success in the corporate world is impossible. Accomplishment in the correct way is always followed by the evidence of money. If we have any doubts about that, all we need to do is ask at the highest or the lowest level of workforce and the substance of the statement will soon be demonstrated. 

Unless variable assessment criteria in terms of market advantage, production, services, and financial expenditure and return are determined for feedback and strategies, it will be like playing football without goalposts, scoreboards or a game plan. Such criteria should be strategically placed within the corporate mission statement. So let's do a corporate assessment and a suggested mission statement for it.
  
**Suggested statement:** *The performance of our product, personnel and investment will be regularly assessed to enable us to address inadequacies.*
  
#### 8. The Corporate Creed
  
Every corporation needs to have a substantial statement of integrity that declares to all concerned the morality that governs its action. Although rarely used or looked at in the past, the ethics of corporations are now being spotlighted by many of the regulatory authorities and the public. An ethics or morality statement from a corporation sets it apart and creates confidence and commitment that can even supersede salaries and opportunity. So let's put in something about a corporate creed with a suggested mission statement.
  
**Suggested statement:** *All corporate activities will respect the ethics of confidence, clarity, and truth, and depend on all those involved to respond with loyalty to the corporate goals and mission*.

### Assessing the Dynamics of Your Mission Statement
  
Now assessing the dynamics of your mission statement, monitoring the mission. As you come towards the end of this lesson, I hope you have grasped the principles I have shared and are now ready to put into action what you have learned. Now I suggest that you lock in some regular time to review each of the principles. 

I am reminded so often of my own ineptness when something goes wrong with a piece of machinery or a special mixture I may use at our cattle farm. The first thing we do to correct the situation is to go back and read the instructions. Inevitably, we find that we have overlooked something. So do what you do when painting your house. If the mixture doesn't do the job, go back and read the instructions on the label of the can.
  
Now let me suggest five easy ways to monitor the continued effectiveness of your dynamic mission statement.
  
  1. **Does everyone feel included?** Isolation for anyone who has a role to play in the outworking of your mission will create blockages. For a personal goal, where the family and friends are involved and needed, everyone who is diffident or unaware about the mission can cause it to flounder. In major corporations and even small businesses or projects, an understanding of a mission statement is essential, and clarity of perception relating to facts needs to be assured.
  
  2. **Has it inspired a culture?** A corporation that tends to form its identity and character from the mission statement as it is expressed and demonstrated by the management sometimes receives cross signals from senior management conflicting with the mission statement. This is sure death to any corporate culture. The tendency is to evaluate a mission statement according to people's value systems, which reflects in an ethical way the reality of their commitment. 
  
  3. **Do outsiders clearly understand it?** The best way to monitor the mission statement is to listen to your customers or supporters, who will mirror the way they see the company they are dealing with. One friend of mine used to disguise his voice and phone his own company, seeking service and understanding of the company's culture, which gave him a direct insight into how his mission was understood by his staff. An individual with a mission can be sure the descriptive responses from other people will provide a guide to its perceived nature. 
  
  4. **Have all the distinctives been covered?** Feedback by way of difficulties caused by omissions can readily identify distinctives that have been overlooked, both personally and corporately. Use caution, however, in making changes especially to your mission statement, because if you insert a distinction that is not a long term distinctive, then you may create more confusion than confidence. Test and try alterations. Only after absolute assessment should you make any change. If you change a number of times, you are suggesting that your mission is a vacillating program rather than a confirming document. 
  
  5. **What is its relationship to the productive output?** The title of this assessment principle seems somewhat superfluous because it describes what seems to be the obvious, but often the obvious can be obscured through argument or circumstances. Continually ask the question, is the mission relating to our productive output in a measurable way?. If not, first look at the adherence of the mission statement, then the vehicle, and finally the goal.
