### A Formula for Handling a Major Financial Crisis

Now I'd like to share with you another formula that I developed over the years called a formula for handling a major financial crisis. In all that I've done in my life, this has saved more lives from suicide than anything else. 

A formula for handling a major crisis has some very definite and definitive principles. 

- Your attitude is far more relative than facts
- Your attitude is far more versatile than circumstances
- Your attitude is far more powerful than opposition
- Your attitude is far more resilient than pressure
- Your attitude is far more flexible than time
- Your attitude is far more valuable than money
- Your attitude is far more influential than positioning
- Your attitude is far more forceful than markets

1. **WRITE DOWN THE WORST KNOWN FACTS AND FEARS AND THEIR COSTS** 
	- Write down the worst known facts and fears and their cost.
	- Taking control of your attitude and understanding the timing and documenting the facts.
	- Accept total responsibility for the situation and think positively with a cheerful disposition.

2. **GETALONE AND ROLE PLAY PASTVICTORIES OVER DIFFICULTIES** 
	- Remember that everybody has failures and difficulties.
	- Go for a walk along the beach and clear your mind.
	- Have a victory book and write down what you have done well in your life and read it aloud. Looking back on victories you can gain strength.
	- Read biographies.

3. **A THREAT PERCEIVED IS MORE HORRIBLE THAN A THREAT PERFORMED, SO PUT THE THREAT INTO PERSPECTIVE** 
	- Problems are always magnified under pressure.
	- Nothing is final, not even death
	- Threats test your ethics!!!!
	- The threat you are facing is horrible - compared to what?

4. **PLAN A COUNTDOWN**
	- You have two choices: let it happen or make it happen. So try and control the happening, and remember God's greatest greatest power gift to you is the gift of choice.
	- You are not a slave to attorneys or governments or creditors.
	- Work out a planned time frame.
	- Retain your dignity, and let those who oppose you know you are doing your best.

5. **EXTEND THE DEADLINE** 
	- Improve relationships and make friends with your opponents.
	- Reduce expenditure and create a survival budget.
	- Extend time frames to allow for solutions.
	- Never accept that you are in a non-negotiable situation.

6. **DIVIDE AND CONQUER IS A GOOD BATTLE STRATEGY** 
	- Remember that all problems are made up of component parts.
	- Dig deep and find opportunities to divide the big problems into bite sized chunks.
	- Every small problem you solve reduces the whole.

7. **WORK ON A BACK-UP PLAN FOR AFTER THE EVENT** 
	- Don't wait for the wind-down to wind-up.
	- Think of the future while your brain juices are perking and not during the rest period.

8. **PHONE SIX PEOPLE YOU KNOW AND ASK THEM WHAT THEIR BIGGEST PROBLEM WAS AND HOW THEY HANDLED IT!** 
	- Whatever you do, don't tell them your problem first, and you will find that their story will wash away emotional debris. Afterwards, ask them how they would handle yours and compile the answers.
	- They'll put your problem into a better perspective.

9. **REMEMBER THE KETTLE ONLY BOILS WHEN THERE IS WATER IN IT!!!** 
	- The only reason you're getting pressure is because others think it will produce something either monetarily or emotionally. 
	- Tell them, 'If you crush me I can't produce, but if you release me, I may get rich and pay you.'

10. **DON'T BE SIDE-TRACKED INTO FOOLISH DECISIONS**
	- Be careful of suggested schemes to make you perform by going in deeper. Keep your mind on logic.
	- Circumstances must not force you. You are in control.
	- Keep short reins on your moral obligations.

11. **DON'T BE PRESSURED INTO UNWISE MOVES** 
	- Do not let your bank or creditors pressure you, and do not sign anything under duress.
	- Be very careful of advice from well meaning family members or friends who do not fully understand the situation. 
	- It's your life. Control it. Use a formula for handling stress.

12. **GO ON THE ATTACK** 
	- Work at it continually. Don't be childlike and hide under the blankets.
	- Be innovative in your thinking and give people options.
	- Keep your eye on the countdown.

13. **KEEP USING YOUR TIME** 
	- Don't run out of puff. Create a mental second wind.
	- Don't allow waiting time to be wasting time.
	- Use any delays for a habit clean up and a disciplined assessment for reassessing your future.

14. **KEEP SOME FUNDS IN RESERVE TO PRIME THE PUMP** 1

### A Formular for Developing the Power of an Idea

Now let's move along to something a little different. From time to time you're going to have an idea and you're going to wonder how to develop it. And for this I give you my formula for developing the power of an idea. It has seven simple principles.

1. **It Must Be Practical:** Many ideas are theoretical and never stand the test of pragmatism. They don't work. Make your idea specific and measurable. Ask the question, 'Is it economically feasible?' Is it practical?. And remember that it must solve a real problem or meet a real need.

2. **It Must Have Purpose:** The idea must enrich all participants. Make sure it's not empty. It must have human and economic substance. Describe clearly what form it takes.

3. **It Must Conform To A Plan:** Is the plan to build an economic army of men and women that will encompass the whole world?. Or how is it marketable?

4. **It Must Be Precise:** The timing must be right. Is this an idea whose time has come? Does it have a time frame? If so, what is it?

5. **It Must Be Personalized:** Will it fulfill the individual dreams and aspirations of others, and will it be respected? Be pragmatic and ask yourself the question, 'Does it relate to an individual or a collective need?'

6. **It Must Become Popular:** Can you utilize a multiplication factor? In other words, can it be cloned?

7. **It Must Have Permanence:** Will it be suitable for the long haul? Create a charter that is clear and make your mission statement firm. The direction must be sure. Remember that the people who are involved are its strength. Have you developed a track record to justify your belief in its success.
