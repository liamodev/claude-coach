# Lesson One: The Dream
  
**The Dream - The sense of grandeur towards life.**

**Biblical Base for Dreams:**
Ecclesiastes 5:3 'For a dream comes through much activity ... and a fool's voice is known by his many words.'
Acts 2: 17 'And it shall come to pass in the last days, says God, that I will pour out my spirit on all flesh: your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your young men shall see visions, your old men shall dream dreams.'
Ecclesiastes 5:7 'For in the multitude of dreams and many words there is also vanity. But fear God!'
  
Every human being is given by God certain attributes which can be nurtured, neglected, or destroyed. One of these is the capacity to dream. 

All of us at some time or other have dreamed of something great or wonderful happening in our life. When we were young, the dreams were often fanciful, but nonetheless sometimes wonderful and sometimes horrible. 

Dreaming about many different things when you are young is incontestable as breath itself. The fact that you dream illustrates your hidden capacities and your unawakened abilities and creates, if selectively promoted, a sense of grandeur towards life. Because when you dream, you're on the periphery of destiny itself. So the first glimpse of destiny comes in a myriad of dreams, of what is not or what could be. As infantile and fanciful as some dreams appear to be, they are the necessary elementary step towards more rational selective pursuits. 

The next step in selectivity appears to be more inward than outward. Let me explain. Life tends to be more about what you decide to commit yourself to than about external influences. Many people wait a lifetime for an opportunity to prevent itself. Others out in the arena of life are fully involved and consumed by what they perceive their dream to be. They are the ones who are much more likely to achieve their vision.
  
The next step is a little more involved because the dreams that people have are usually mobile and dependent on feelings, locations, and personal circumstances at any given time. The easiest and most profound way to assess your dream is on the basis of its destiny qualities and what you are prepared to exchange it for in measurable form.

In days gone by, they called some dreams pipe dreams, a term which originated from opium smoking, where the illusions would come and dissipate in a cloud of smoke, finally to be replaced by yet another dream that would vanish in the same way. 
### Action vs. Words

For some, the act of dreaming becomes an end in itself, and dreams are pursued only by words. Again, the Bible in the book of Ecclesiastes gives us a great truth when it says, 'For a dream comes through much activity, and a fool's voice is known by his many words', indicating that if you want to realize your dream, you must be active. But if all you do is talk, not much will happen. Just talking about a dream is folly. 

Remember, of course, that dreaming is the only the beginning and is there to provide many panoramic views of the grandeur of life, to be a stimulus, not a finale. 

Many dreams are frivolous or lighthearted or fantasies which are useful to escape from reality. However, the acceptance that life is and can be a grand event with a measure of permanence creates an awesome impact on those who follow after us.

Great dreams are not instant. Very often, they are hard won. Some are modified by effort, focus, and fortune, and the capacity of the dreamer.

Sir Winston Churchill did not achieve his dream until he was about to retire. Martin Luther King and Mahatma Gandhi were assassinated before their dreams were fulfilled. The cost of achieving a dream, if pursued with serious intent, is the same for a big dream or a small dream or no dream at all. It is the sum total of your life. 

A dream realized can have long term benefits for the world. In those who refuse to dream, the spirit responds by drying up. There are none so poor as those unfortunate beings with no spirit and no dream.

### A Five-Step Plan for Life Dreams

If you have lost the capacity to dream about your future and the incredible possibilities you can achieve, then I would suggest a five step plan for your life.

1. **Read Biographies:** Read biographies of great achievers. If you have difficulty reading, then obtain audiobooks which are readily available today on these subjects. I have read over 5,000 biographies and made notes from them towards my own destiny.

2. **Embrace Courage:** Embrace courage as your friend and put your life up for scrutiny by becoming involved in those things which you have thus far feared to do. There is no need to risk life and limb, but expose yourself situations that will stretch your mental courage and capacity.

3. **Consider a Dynamic Dream:** Consider a dream with an impact so dynamic that it will last at least a hundred years after you have expired. Remember, of course, that it is at this stage only a dream. The rest of this program will give you the principles to validate and, if possible, achieve its fulfillment.

4. **Develop Inspirational Dissatisfaction:** Develop an inspiration dissatisfaction with the status quo, fulfilling all the obligations of your personal responsibilities, but eagerly anticipating anticipating what is to come.

5. **Commit to the Program:** Make the commitment to move on to the next lesson, and finally complete the whole program with serious intent. Understand, of course, that dreams have always been the catalyst of man's highest hopes and a reflection of our deepest needs, and that they arrive unexpectedly and at times disappear without so much as a trace, like slippery fish that if not possessed with a vice like grip, can quickly vanish. 

Be careful with whom you choose to share your dreams, because dreams shared with others can be met with disbelief, humor, and even ridicule. And the discouragement can cause doubt and sometimes abandonment of a very worthy endeavor. In my own particular case, I did not share my dreams for over twenty five years because I was sure there was no one who could understand my commitment and focus level.

### Six Principles to Identify Dream Stealers

So now let's move forward as I give you six principles to identify dream stealers. These are things or people that come in and take your dreams away so that they will never be fulfilled.

1. **Lack of Focus:** Consider the dream that you want realized. Can you see the full picture, and are you able to verbalize it clearly? The best way to focus it is to reduce it to written form with measurements applying to each part.

2. **Fear of the Unknown:** Fear expresses itself in many forms. The most prevalent is indecisiveness, and the only solution to indecisiveness is clarity. There will always be unknowns, but indecisiveness magnifies doubt and disappears once there is a firm resolve to move ahead, irrespective of obstacles.

3. **Admit and Learn by Mistakes:** Every mistake has its own penalty. Every mistake that is not admitted and corrected will delay your dream.

4. **Inconsistent Work Habits:** Routine and good work habits are disciplines that provide the oil to help your dream run smoothly. Break the bad habit syndrome, or you'll provide an opportunity for the dream thief to enter.

5. **Create Good Relationships:** Being at peace with all mankind is a lofty ideal and a worthy ambition. The people you do not need today may be of pivotal importance tomorrow, and having many friends ensures a pleasant pathway of life. Broken and bad relationships build up enmity and burn up dreams and energy.

6. **Financial Security:** It becomes more difficult to dream while you're under financial stress unless you have unusual focus. My strong advice to you is to live within your means. If you do not save, the seeds of success are not in you.

### Faith and Imagination

As the years progressed in my life, I studied thought technology and behavioral sciences. I am convinced beyond any shadow of a doubt that the subconscious mind cannot distinguish between a real or an imagined act. Dreams are the only property of substance that is created from nothingness, and they confirm and affirm the early biblical record that man is made in the likeness of his Creator. 

There is no reason for God to give you the capacity to imagine and to dream positively and creatively without him expecting you to realize a dream and complete the task.

To ignite a dream requires a participant to think the unthinkable, to relate to the unrelatable, and to give in human terms, sometimes the ungivable. 

The birth of your dream usually comes from unconscious needs, so don't force it, don't limit it, and don't copy it from others and thereby lose your own identity and ownership.

### Examining the Substance of Your Dream

Here are four simple questions for examining the substance of your dream.

1. Do you want to impact your area, your state, your country, your region, or the whole world?

2. By what means will you express that impact?

3. How will you accurately measure its effect?

4. By what method can you guarantee the continuance or permanence of your life's work?
   
The acid test of the dream is its overwhelming, all encompassing, and demonstrable response from the possessor.

Frivolous dreams of great or little consequence exhibit shallowness by erratic and changing performance. 

Serious dreams are the substance of greatness. Through the ages, men and women of little stature have arrested the attention of the whole world by their fervent passion for progress. 

All dreams of any consequence to be realized should be evaluated in terms of passion, perseverance, and permanence. Their greatness can easily be measured by their impact. Never discard a good idea because your ideas are the beginnings of dreams for you personally to achieve and not for someone else. To subject and submit yourself to someone else's dream can destroy for you opportunities for greatness.

### Five Questions to Expand Your Thinking

Consider answering the five following questions to expand your thinking process.

1. What do you believe to be the three most driving and controlling influences that determine the continuation of the universe? 

2. Name three limiting factors in your life?

3. Rate in order of importance, body, mind, and spirit?

4. Do you believe that you have a bad, good, or excellent excellent opportunity to develop a meaningful contribution to the world?

5. If you were to give someone else advice about being successful, what are the three most important principles you would share with them?

I believe that there are no dull minds, only dormant minds, and all that is necessary to prove the point is to activate the switch.

What I'm going to do now is to provide some simple insights into yourself that will show you clearly just how powerful your creative capacity is and the strength of the energy that it produces.

Take your thoughts back, not to an imaginary, but to a real experience that caused you anxiety and maybe even fear. It could be a nightmare, a personal disappointment over a relationship, or some criticism or unkind remark that was made about you. 

Reflect again on the thoughts that were paramount in your mind at that time, and you will find that you imagine all kinds of things. For instance:
- Why did they say that?
- What will happen to me now?
- I wonder what they meant by that?
- I bet they're going to do such and such?
- What will I do?

You see, you did what all human beings do under stress; you created what we call mind talk. Along with the mind talk, you created imaginary scenes, situations, probabilities, fears, and even sometimes revenge. What is happening in your mind at these times is the creation of word pictures, imaginings, and possibilities. In other words, your past, present, and future word and visionary machinery is operating at particular level of creativity and inspiration, which proves that it is in good functioning order. 

This will provide evidence for the most ardent skeptic that the imagination does operate and is in good working order even at the lowest level of mental capacity. In fact, I would suggest that the machinery of the imagination is in better running order the lower you go down the intellectual ladder. The other side of the coin is that it's probably also more out of control and therefore not as useful as discipline creativity.

The use of the imagination involves our past, present, and future. As well, there are at least two other areas which occupy imagination at other times. They are 'the possible' and 'fantasy'. For instance, I have known people to talk about the past using their imagination and their actual remembered experience, which seems somehow to be shaped into something quite different from what they really were. They use the past tense and then color it with a possible or fanciful outcome. 

Without disrespect to anyone, let me say that all of us indulge in this kind of illusionary deception at some time or other. 

This can also hold true in other areas such as imagining the future and the possible or fantasy dimension, testing mentally as it were the decisions that may have to be made under certain imaginary circumstances.

All of these processes have been used by us without identifying or labeling the experiences, and because we do not identify the experience, but rather use them in a slip shod way, we cannot hope to gain the maximum benefit from them. 

The next step to harnessing the unmeasured capacity of our imagination is to look in general terms at the vertical dimensions or the different layers of our creative possibilities. So let's now go through each one.

#### 1. The Free-Rein Erratic Response

Free-rein erratic response defines a totally out of control imagination. It jumps from fantasy to fact and from the past to the future with no purpose of restraint without remaining long enough to draw out any conclusions. 
This usually happens with insecure but hopeful people. They feel that there must be something mystical about success and achievement, and they hope to stumble upon it. 
The partly dormant but erratic imagination usually belongs to an overly stiff 'prove it to me' person, practical person, or to the fearful type, 'you're hiding something from me', who hold their thoughts so tightly in a rigid protected area of possibilities that the imagination is released only in small bursts at times of fear, protectiveness, or doubt.

#### 2. The Hereditary Imposed Response

Just as our physical features have hereditary components, so do our mental faculties. Television has often shown the story of brothers and sisters who had not known the existence of one another and through some special circumstances. They met late in their lives for the first time and found that they had certain close physical, personality, and mental similarities. 
However, those who are determined to do so can prove beyond any doubt that permanent changes in all of these areas are quite possible. Such changes require predetermined planning and meticulous and continued discipline to overcome the bias towards a particular direction. 
All of us are prone to hereditary imagination responses, but the good news is that we can change if we want to. We no longer have to accept our own family peculiarities as a chain binding and forbidding us to venture up to the next step ladder of life.

#### 3. Relative Response

At this level of imagination, behavior changes with surrounding circumstances and very often changes with the company or prevailing attitude of the moment. The response is another of the wonderful miracles of the mind, which never ceases to function but is dependent upon and related to input. 
The circumstantial circumstantial response of the imagination is almost automatic and is another way in which the energy of the imagination is activated and functions without much control, rarely producing effective or long term results. A relative circumstantial response always depends on the people, circumstances, and other dynamics that are present. The imagination is stimulated by the mood and the person reacts accordingly, thus providing us with another glimpse into the levels of the imagination that from time to time operate in us all.

#### 4. Emotional Relative Response

All of us are faced with the emotional relative response from time to time. We can be joyful, angry, bitter, or sad, and our imagination will respond accordingly, which again demonstrates that it is in good working order. The danger signal flashes when the imagination jumps from one emotional crisis to another or responds quickly or erratically to any given situation and maintain momentum with dramatic word and visionary pictures that exaggerate or have little or no relation to what is actually taking place. 
It is quite natural to imagine all kinds of disasters or benefits when emotion takes over. And as long as this is accepted as a warning system of possibilities and the cause is recognized, then the imagination has done the best job it can do under the circumstances since it cannot control the input.

#### 5. Automatic Experiential Response

Experiences are stored in the mind forever. They can act as a catalyst to expand the imagination in the area of problem solving and futuristic thinking. As all your experiences are stored for reference, protection, and useful implementation, they form a very deep functional part of the creative process. 
This is yet another level in this fascinating subject of the imagination. Your immediate response to any situation can be linked to your past experiences and produce imaginary word and picture images that can help or hurt the situation at hand.
What makes a difference is whether or not your experiences are positively important to you in your conscious everyday thought. If they are and you seek to learn from them, it follows logically that your imagination will pick up the importance of each experience and respond accordingly when thoughts, words, and images are required. The emphasis here should be on learning from experience. Ask yourself the question, 'What is the lesson here?' If you are really serious, you would document it and review it from time to time.

#### 6. Principle Controlled Response

It is rare to find someone who has no principles at all. People may have many or few principles, and these may differ considerably. But the principles they do have tend to govern their lives, the most influential principle emanating from the person's dominant reason for existence. 
These principles can be rigid, bent, or discarded relative to degree or desire or fear at any given moment, and depending on the degree of integrity of the individual. 
The principles that represents a person's public or private life may have quite different faces and conflicting directions. The imagination functions in both the public and private realms of behavior and is controlled or stimulated by the basic principles that an individual truly believes in. 
For instance, it could be that publicly a person talks about the principles of honesty and integrity. But privately, the same person's principles allow the use of any means to justify a specific end, including creative accountancy, tax evasion, dishonest or illegal practices, situation ethics. 
The imagination will follow the leaning most convenient to the person concerned. The other important fact here is that your children and those who are close enough to observe you will recognize a real you as you express and pursue ideas and dreams.

The understanding that we gain from each level provides for us opportunities to analyze and upgrade our talents, and at the same time, gives us directional control to provide a predetermined stimulant to our imagination. #### 8. Automatic Selective Flow

As you become more secure in the use of your creative abilities, so your imagination becomes more sophisticated in its ability to search out and provide more answers and questions about what is needed. To graduate to this level with some permanent result, there must be an understanding of an ability at the previous level of the destiny directed imaging.

Most of the previously discussed levels in imageology have been dependent on either conscious reactionary stimuli or subjected to controlled input. This provides the foundation for the next step, automatic selected flow.

Just as the title suggests, automatic cycle active flow is not dependent on disciplined stimuli, but rather on a total automatic search system. It investigates all the previous levels without stimuli or a program, and gives insights, ideas, and alternatives as a common occurrence in almost every situation.

This level is particularly pleasing, and it can become so reliable that it almost seemed to become a habit because of its speed and continuity.

We all have automatic selective flow in a limited form. We experience it from time to time and enjoy the exhilaration of surprise and wonder at the information it produces. But here, we are talking about a permanent automatic flow that requires no stimuli other than an active disciplined mind and a progressive positive attitude.

Relax and enjoy this level because through it, you can enhance your own life and encourage, direct, and help those around you.

#### 9. Flash Imageology

Everyone at some time or other experiences a flash of information in the mind that suits a purpose at hand. It can provide information or ideas that leave us wondering how they came. Many great scientific and literary giants trace their great thoughts and discoveries back to a single flash of an idea from which further thoughts, ideas, and possibilities spread out like tentacles of an octopus. 

Although we look at the greats of the past and present and admire their insights and achievements, we must focus on the dynamics rather than the results if we are to benefit and use the process ourselves.

Those who create and have insights at what we may consider the genius level from time to time experience extraordinary flash that captures their thought patterns in ways beyond the normal. While it's impossible to give a full and thorough thesis on the way that flash works without reference and counter reference, together with case studies and other evidence, I believe that there are some simple fundamentals that apply.

3. A continually informed mind regarding the object of your mission is essential.

4. Accept all ideas and thoughts available before making a judgment.

### Five Absolutes That Work

There are five absolutes that work.

- **Belief in Direction:** There is no point in applying your imagination as something that you do not completely believe in because you are severely restricting and in some cases negating that which you claim you want.

- **Belief in Purpose:** You can believe you're going in the right direction just because it makes good common sense.

- **Belief in Response:** Only a fool would work towards something without expecting it to get or do that which was specifically required. Your belief that you will get a response must be unswerving.

- **Belief in Multiplicity:** Expect and receive the add-on system of responses from your imagination and do not stop the flow for deep analysis. Otherwise, you may cut off the supply and thereby limit further information. Understand that ideas and thoughts feed and expand on one another.

- **Belief in Totality:** Do not accept half an answer, but rather persist in believing and imagining imagining until a full picture presents itself.

We do know, however, that the more you combine a strong belief in an idea with the input of informed thought and consistent stimulation, the more likely it is that the answer will pop into your mind under the shower or a bed at night or when you are least expecting it. Do not necessarily accept the first impression as final, but rather write it down and refer to it later for add on ideas.

Consider also the possibility of using imageology in exactly the opposite direction. That is, presuppose you are searching for a clue to stop a project. Then ask the question, 'What would I really want to happen?' Thereby, you can obtain another valuable insight into the fascinating world of imageology.
